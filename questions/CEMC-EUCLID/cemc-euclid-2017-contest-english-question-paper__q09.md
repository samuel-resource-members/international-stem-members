```text
9.     A permutation of a list of numbers is an ordered arrangement of the numbers in
         that list. For example, 3, 2, 4, 1, 6, 5 is a permutation of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. We can write
          this permutation as a1, a2, a3, a4, a5, a6, where a1 = 3, a2 = 2, a3 = 4, a4 = 1, a5 = 6,
        and a6 = 5.

          (a) Determine the average value of

                                               |a1 −a2| + |a3 −a4|

             over all permutations a1, a2, a3, a4 of 1, 2, 3, 4.

         (b) Determine the average value of

                                   a1 −a2 + a3 −a4 + a5 −a6 + a7

             over all permutations a1, a2, a3, a4, a5, a6, a7 of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

          (c) Determine the average value of

                      |a1 −a2| + |a3 −a4| + · · · + |a197 −a198| + |a199 −a200|         (∗)

             over all permutations a1, a2, a3, ..., a199, a200 of 1, 2, 3, 4, . . . , 199, 200. (The sum
              labelled (∗) contains 100 terms of the form |a2k−1 −a2k|.)
```
