21. An empty freight car on a level railroad track has a mass M. A chute above the freight car opens and
    grain falls down into the car at a rate of r, measured in kilograms per second. The grain falls a vertical
    distance h before hitting the bed of the freight car without bouncing up. How much normal force is
    exerted on the freight car from the rails at a time t after the grain begins to hit the bed of the car?
   Assume the grain starts from rest.

         (A) Mg + rtg
         (B) Mg + r√gh
         (C) Mg + r√2gh
         (D) Mg + r√gh + rtg
         (E) Mg + r√2gh + rtg
