24. A ball of mass m moving at speed v collides with a massless spring of spring constant k mounted on a
    stationary box of mass M in free space. No mechanical energy is lost in the collision. If the system does
    not rotate, what is the maximum compression x of the spring?

         q mM         (A) x = v                   (m+M)k
         (B) x = vp m                         k
         q M         (C) x = v                         k

         q m+M         (D) x = v                           k

         q (m+M)3
