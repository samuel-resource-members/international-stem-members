2. A mass m hangs from a massless spring connected to the roof of a box of mass M. When the box is held
   stationary, the mass-spring system oscillates vertically with angular frequency ω.  If the box is dropped
    falls freely under gravity, how will the angular frequency change?

        (A) ω will be unchanged

        (B) ω will increase

        (C) ω will decrease

        (D) Oscillations are impossible under these conditions

        (E) ω will increase or decrease depending on the values of M and m
