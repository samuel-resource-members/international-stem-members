15. A car of mass m has an engine that provides a constant power output P.  Assuming no friction, what is the
   maximum constant speed vmax that this car can drive up a long incline that makes an angle θ with the horizontal?

         (A) vmax = P/(mg sin θ)
         (B) vmax = P 2 sin θ/mg
         (C) vmax = p 2P/mg/ sin θ

         (D) There is no maximum constant speed.

         (E) The maximum constant speed depends on the length of the incline.
