24. A thin sheet of air with density ρ is blown at a uniform speed v toward a smooth, convex cylindrical
    surface of radius R. The sheet has uniform thickness t ≪R and remains everywhere tangent to the
    cylinder while it stays attached. Neglect gravity and viscosity.

   The pressure on the outer side of the air sheet is constant and equal to P. The pressure on the side
    adjacent to the cylinder is lower by an amount ∆P. The air sheet follows the curvature of the cylinder
    until it can no longer remain attached.

   Given the parameters below, what is the maximum integer speed vmax for which the sheet can remain
    attached to the surface?
