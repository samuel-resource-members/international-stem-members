(E) 2v0

The following information applies to questions 9 and 10

A 0.650 kg ball moving at 5.00 m/s collides with a 0.750 kg ball that is originally at rest. After
the collision, the 0.750 kg ball moves oﬀwith a speed of 4.00 m/s, and the 0.650 kg ball moves oﬀ
at a right angle to the ﬁnal direction of motion of the 0.750 kg ball.

10. Let the change in total kinetic energy in this collision be deﬁned by ∆K = Kf −Ki, where Kf is
    the total ﬁnal kinetic energy, and Ki is the total initial kinetic energy. Which of the following is
    true?

         (A) ∆K = (Ki + Kf)/2

         (B) Kf < ∆K < Ki

         (C) 0 < ∆K < Kf

         (D) ∆K = 0

         (E) −Ki < ∆K < 0
