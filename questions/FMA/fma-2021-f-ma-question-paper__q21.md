```text
21. A solid sphere sits at the top of a ramp of height h inclined at angle θ to the horizontal. Both the
    static and kinetic coeﬃcients of friction between the sphere and incline are µk = µs = 0.2. The
    sphere is released from rest at the top of the incline. For which of the following values of θ is the
    total translational plus rotational kinetic energy of the sphere greatest when it reaches the bottom
    of the incline?

    (A) 10◦
    (B) 45◦
    (C) 60◦
    (D) 80◦

    (E) The mechanical energy is the same for all choices.
```
