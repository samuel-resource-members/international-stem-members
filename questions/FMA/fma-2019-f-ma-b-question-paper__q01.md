1. A uniform block of mass 10 kg is released at rest from the top of an incline of length 10 m and inclination
   30◦. The coeﬃcients of static and kinetic friction between the incline and the block are µs = 0.15 and
   µk = 0.1. The end of the incline is connected to a frictionless horizontal surface. After a long time, how
  much energy is dissipated due to friction?

        (A) 75 J

        (B) 87 J

        (C) 130 J

        (D) 147 J

        (E) 500 J
