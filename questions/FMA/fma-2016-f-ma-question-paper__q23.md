```text
23. A uniform thin circular rubber band of mass M and spring constant k has an original radius R.
   Now it is tossed into the air. Assume it remains circular when stabilized in air and rotates at
    angular speed ω about its center uniformly. Which of the following gives the new radius of the
    rubber band?

         (A) (2πkR)/(2πk −Mω2)
         (B) (4πkR)/(4πk −Mω2)
         (C) (8π2kR)/(8π2k −Mω2)
         (D) (4π2kR)/(4π2k −Mω2)
         (E) (4πkR)/(2πk −Mω2)
```
