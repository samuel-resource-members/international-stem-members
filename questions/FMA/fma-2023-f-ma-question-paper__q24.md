24. A very heavy plate continuously moves up and down with a small speed v, switching directions after each
    time t. At a random time, a ball is dropped from rest far above the plate, then bounces elastically off of
      it. How does the ball’s speed change during this collision?


    (A) The ball always slows down.

    (B) The ball is more likely to slow down than to speed up.

    (C) The ball is equally likely to slow down or speed up.

    (D) The ball is more likely to speed up than to slow down.

    (E) The ball always speeds up.
