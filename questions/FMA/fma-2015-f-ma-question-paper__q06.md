6. Three trolley carts are free to move on a one dimensional frictionless horizontal track. Cart A has
   a mass of 1.9 kg and an initial speed of 1.7 m/s to the right; Cart B has a mass of 1.1 kg and an
   initial speed of 2.5 m/s to the left; cart C has a mass of 1.3 kg and is originally at rest. Collisions
   between carts A and B are perfectly elastic; collisions between carts B and C are perfectly inelastic.

                           Cart A         Cart B         Cart C
                                1.9 kg            1.1 kg            1.3 kg


                               1.7 m/s          2.5 m/s


  What is the velocity of the center of mass of the system of the three carts after the last collision?

        (A) 0.11 m/s

        (B) 0.16 m/s

        (C) 1.4 m/s

        (D) 2.0 m/s

        (E) 3.23 m/s
