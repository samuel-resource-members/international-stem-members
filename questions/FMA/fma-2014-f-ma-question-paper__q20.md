20. A crew of scientists has built a new space station. The space station is shaped like a wheel of
    radius R, with essentially all its mass M at the rim. When the crew arrives, the station will be set
    rotating at a rate that causes an object at the rim to have radial acceleration g, thereby simulating
    Earth’s surface gravity. This is accomplished by two small rockets, each with thrust T newtons,
   mounted on the station’s rim. How long a time t does one need to ﬁre the rockets to achieve the
    desired condition?

         (A) t = pgR3 M/(2T)
         (B) t = √gR M/(2T)
         (C) t = √gR M/T
         (D) t = pgR/π M/T
         (E) t = √gR M/(πT)

   Adapted from a problem in Physics for Scientists and Engineers by Richard Wolfson
