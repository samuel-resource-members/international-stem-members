13. A massless cable of diameter 2.54 cm (1 inch) is tied horizontally between two trees 18.0 m apart. A
    tightrope walker stands at the center of the cable, giving it a tension of 7300 N. The cable stretches and
   makes an angle of 1.50◦with the horizontal.





                                                1.5o





   The Young’s modulus is deﬁned as the ratio of stress to strain, where stress is the force applied per unit
    area and strain is the fractional change in length ∆L/L. The cable’s Young’s modulus is

         (A) 1.5 × 106 N/m2
         (B) 2.0 × 108 N/m2
         (C) 2.2 × 109 N/m2
         (D) 2.4 × 1010 N/m2
         (E) 4.2 × 1010 N/m2
