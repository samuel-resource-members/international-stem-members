13. A uniform box with mass m is at rest on a horizontal surface, and the coefficient of static friction between
   them is µs. A force directed at an angle of 85◦above the horizontal is applied to the center of the box,
    with a linearly increasing magnitude F = βt.

                                      F





   The box will eventually slide or lift off the ground. Which of the following is correct?

    (A) If µs < tan 85◦, the box will lift off the ground first.
    (B) If µs < tan 85◦, the box will slide first.
    (C) For any value of µs, the box will lift off the ground first.
    (D) For any value of µs, the box will slide first.

    (E) The answer depends on the values of β, g, and m.
