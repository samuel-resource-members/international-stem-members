19. A small rock is tied to a massless string of length 5 m. The density of the rock is twice the density of
    the water. The rock is lowered into the water, while the other end of the string is attached to a pivot.
    Neglect any resistive forces from the water. The rock oscillates like a pendulum with angular frequency
    of which of the following?

         (A) 1 rad/s

         (B) 0.7 rad/s

         (C) 0.5 rad/s

         (D) 1.4 rad/s

         (E) 2 rad/s
