20. A well-calibrated scale reads zero when nothing is placed on it. When a deﬂated balloon is placed
   on the scale, it reads mg. When a non-airtight box is placed on the scale, it reads Mg, where
  M > m. The balloon is inﬂated with helium, so that it ﬂoats upward in air. The balloon is placed
    in the box, and the box is placed on the scale. If the scale reads W, which of the following is true?

    (A) W ≤(M −m)g

    (B) (M −m)g < W < (M + m)g

    (C) W = (M + m)g

    (D) W > (M + m)g

    (E) None of the above are necessarily true.
