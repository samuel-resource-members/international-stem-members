## Text (OCR)

```text
23. Consider two uniform spherical planets of equal density but unequal radius. Which of the following
    quantities is the same for both planets?

           (a) The escape velocity from the planet’s surface.

          (b) The acceleration due to gravity at the planet’s surface.

            (c) The orbital period of a satellite in a circular orbit just above the planet’s surface.

          (d) The orbital period of a satellite in a circular orbit at a given distance from the planet’s
                center.

            (e) None of the above.
```
