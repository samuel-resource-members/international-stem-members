12. A group of students wish to measure the acceleration of gravity with a simple pendulum. They take one
    length measurement of the pendulum to be L = 1.00 ± 0.05 m. They then measure the period of a single
    swing to be T = 2.00 ± 0.10 s. Assume that all uncertainties are Gaussian. The computed acceleration
    of gravity from this experiment illustrating the range of possible values should be recorded as

         (A) 9.87 ± 0.10 m/s2
         (B) 9.87 ± 0.15 m/s2
         (C) 9.9 ± 0.25 m/s2
         (D) 9.9 ± 1.1 m/s2
         (E) 9.9 ± 1.5 m/s2
