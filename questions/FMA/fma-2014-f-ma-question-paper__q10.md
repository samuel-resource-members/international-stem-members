10. A radio controlled car is attached to a stake in the ground by a 3.00 m long piece of string, and
     is forced to move in a circular path. The car has an initial angular velocity of 1.00 rad/s and
    smoothly accelerates at a rate of 4.00 rad/s2. The string will break if the centripetal acceleration
    exceeds 2.43 × 102 m/s2. How long can the car accelerate at this rate before the string breaks?

         (C) 1.00 s

         (D) 1.50 s

         (E) 2.00 s
