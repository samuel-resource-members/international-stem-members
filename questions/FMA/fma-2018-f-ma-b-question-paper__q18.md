## Text (OCR)

```text
18. A spring of relaxed length ℓ1 and spring constant k1 is placed ‘in parallel’ with a spring of relaxed length
    ℓ2 and spring constant k2. A force F is applied to each end.


                                                Spring 1
                    F                                   F

                                                Spring 2


   The combination of the springs acts like a single spring with spring constant k and relaxed length ℓwhere

         (A) k = k1 + k2   and  ℓ= ℓ1ℓ2/(ℓ1 + ℓ2)
         (B) k = k1 + k2   and  ℓ= (ℓ1k1 + ℓ2k2)/(k1 + k2)
         (C) k = k1 + k2   and  ℓ= (ℓ1k2 + ℓ2k1)/(k1 + k2)
         (D) k = (ℓ1k1 + ℓ2k2)/(ℓ1 + ℓ2)   and  ℓ= (ℓ1k1 + ℓ2k2)/(k1 + k2)
         (E) k = (ℓ2k1 + ℓ1k2)/(ℓ1 + ℓ2)   and  ℓ= (ℓ1k2 + ℓ2k1)/(k1 + k2)
```
