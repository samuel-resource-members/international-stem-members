24. A mass is attached to one end of a rigid rod, while the other end of the rod is attached to a ﬁxed
    horizontal axle. Initially the mass hangs at the end of the rod and the rod is vertical. The mass is given
   an initial kinetic energy K. If K is very small, the mass behaves like a pendulum, performing small-angle

         (B) increases, approaching a ﬁnite constant.

         (C) decreases, approaching a ﬁnite non-zero constant.

         (D) decreases, approaching zero.
