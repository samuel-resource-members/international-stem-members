12. A spherical shell of mass M and radius R is completely ﬁlled with a frictionless ﬂuid, also of mass M. It is released
    from rest, and then it rolls without slipping down an incline that makes an angle θ with the horizontal. What will
   be the acceleration of the shell down the incline just after it is released? Assume the acceleration of free fall is g.
   The moment of inertia of a thin shell of radius r and mass m about the center of mass is I = 23mr2; the moment
    of inertia of a solid sphere of radius r and mass m about the center of mass is I = 5mr2.2

         (A) a = g sin θ
         (B) a = 34g sin θ
         (C) a = 12g sin θ
         (D) a = 38g sin θ
         (E) a = 35g sin θ
