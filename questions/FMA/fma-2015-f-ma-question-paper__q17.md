17. A ﬂywheel can rotate in order to store kinetic energy. The ﬂywheel is a uniform disk made of a
    material with a density ρ and tensile strength σ (measured in Pascals), a radius r, and a thickness
    h. The ﬂywheel is rotating at the maximum possible angular velocity so that it does not break.
   Which of the following expression correctly gives the maximum kinetic energy per kilogram that
    can be stored in the ﬂywheel? Assume that α is a dimensionless constant.

         (A) α pρσ/r

         (B) αhpρσ/r

         (C) α p(h/r)(σ/ρ)2

         (D) α(h/r)(σ/ρ)

         (E) ασ/ρ
