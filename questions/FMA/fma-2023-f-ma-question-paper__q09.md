## Text (OCR)

```text
9. A helium balloon is released from the floor in a room at rest, then slowly rises and comes to rest touching
   the ceiling. During this process, the gravitational potential energy of the balloon has increased. Since
   energy is conserved, the energy of something else must have decreased during this process. Which of the
   following is the main contribution to this decrease?


   (A) The kinetic energy of the balloon decreased.

   (B) The elastic potential energy of the balloon decreased.

   (C) The thermal energy of the air in the balloon decreased.

   (D) The thermal energy of the air in the room decreased.

   (E) The gravitational potential energy of the air in the room decreased.
```
