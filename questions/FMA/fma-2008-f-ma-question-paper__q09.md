9. A ball of mass m1 travels along the x-axis in the positive direction with an initial speed of v0. It
   collides with a ball of mass m2 that is originally at rest. After the collision, the ball of mass m1
   has velocity v1xˆx + v1yˆy and the ball of mass m2 has velocity v2xˆx + v2yˆy.

   Consider the following ﬁve statements:

     I)    0 = m1v1x + m1v2x
     II)   m1v0 = m1v1y + m2v2y
     III)  0 = m1v1y + m2v2y
    IV)  m1v0 = m1v1x + m1v1y
   V)   m1v0 = m1v1x + m2v2x
  Of these ﬁve statements, the system must satisfy

          (a) I and II

         (b) III and V

           (c) II and V

         (d) III and IV

           (e) I and III
