```text
11. The power output from a certain experimental car design to be shaped like a cube is proportional
    to the mass m of the car. The force of air friction on the car is proportional to Av2, where v is the
    speed of the car and A the cross sectional area. On a level surface the car has a maximum speed
    vmax. Assuming that all versions of this design have the same density, then which of the following
     is true?

         (A) vmax ∝m1/9
         (B) vmax ∝m1/7
         (C) vmax ∝m1/3
         (D) vmax ∝m2/3
         (E) vmax ∝m3/4
```
