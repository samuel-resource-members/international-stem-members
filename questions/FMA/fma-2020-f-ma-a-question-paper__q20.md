```text
20. A car is driving against the wind at a constant speed v0 relative to the ground. The wind direction
     is always opposite to the car’s velocity, but its speed ﬂuctuates about an average speed of v relative
    to the ground. The air drag force is Av2rel, where A is a constant and vrel is the relative speed
    between the car and the wind. What is the average rate P of energy dissipation due to the air
    resistance?

    (A) P = Av0(v0 + v)2
    (B) P > Av0(v0 + v)2
    (C) P < Av0(v0 + v)2

    (D) Both (B) and (C) are possible depending on how v ﬂuctuates.

    (E) Both (A) and (C) are possible depending on how v ﬂuctuates.
```
