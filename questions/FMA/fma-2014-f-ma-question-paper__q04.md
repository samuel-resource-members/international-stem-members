```text
4. What are the correct values of the numbers in the following statements? Assume there are no
   external forces, and take N = 1 to mean that the statement cannot be made for any meaningful
  number of particles.

          •  If a particle at rest explodes into N1 or fewer particles with known masses, and the
               total kinetic energy of the new particles is known, the kinetic energy of each of the new
               particles is completely determined.

          •  If a particle at rest explodes into N2 or fewer particles, the velocities of the new particles
           must lie in a line.

          •  If a particle at rest explodes into N3 or fewer particles, the velocities of the new particles
           must lie in a plane.

        (A) N1 = 2, N2 = 1, N3 = 1

        (B) N1 = 1, N2 = 2, N3 = 3

        (C) N1 = 2, N2 = 2, N3 = 3

        (D) N1 = 3, N2 = 2, N3 = 3

        (E) N1 = 2, N2 = 3, N3 = 4
```
