22. A body of mass M and a body of mass m ≪M are in circular orbits about their center of mass
    under the inﬂuence of their mutual gravitational attraction to each other. The distance between
    the bodies is R, which is much larger than the size of either body.

  A small amount of matter δm ≪m is removed from the body of mass m and transferred to the
   body of mass M. The transfer is done in such a way so that the orbits of the two bodies remain
    circular, and remain separated by a distance R. Which of the following statements is correct?

         (A) The gravitational force between the two bodies increases.

         (B) The gravitational force between the two bodies remains constant.

         (C) The total angular momentum of the system increases.

         (D) The total angular momentum of the system remains constant.

         (E) The period of the orbit of two bodies remains constant.
