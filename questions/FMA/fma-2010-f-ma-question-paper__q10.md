```text
10. A block of mass m1 is on top of a block of mass m2. The lower block is on a horizontal surface, and a rope can
    pull horizontally on the lower block. The coeﬃcient of kinetic friction for all surfaces is µ. What is the resulting
    acceleration of the lower block if a force F is applied to the rope? Assume that F is suﬃciently large so that the
    top block slips on the lower block.


                                                        1
                                                  F
                                                        2


         (A) a2 = (F −µg(2m1 + m2))/m2
         (B) a2 = (F −µg(m1 + m2))/m2
         (C) a2 = (F −µg(m1 + 2m2))/m2
         (D) a2 = (F + µg(m1 + m2))/m2
         (E) a2 = (F −µg(m2 −m1))/m2
```
