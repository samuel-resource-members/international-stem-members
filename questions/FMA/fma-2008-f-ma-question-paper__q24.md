24. A ball is launched upward from the ground at an initial vertical speed of v0 and begins bouncing
     vertically. Every time it rebounds, it loses a proportion of the magnitude of its velocity due to the
     inelastic nature of the collision, such that if the speed just before hitting the ground on a bounce is
    v, then the speed just after the bounce is rv, where r < 1 is a constant. Calculate the total length
    of time that the ball remains bouncing, assuming that any time associated with the actual contact
    of the ball with the ground is negligible.


           (a)  2v0g   1                  1−r
          (b)  v0g   r                 1−r
            (c)  2v0g 1−rr
          (d)  2v0g   1                   1−r2
            (e)  2v0g     1                   1+(1−r)2
