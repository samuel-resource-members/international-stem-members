```text
16 Some electric hotplates are metal and do not glow when hot.

    Other hotplates are glass and have a heater underneath that glows red. The heater makes the
    glass hot.

    With both hotplates, there is no air gap between the hotplate and a metal pan placed on the
     hotplate.

   Which statement is correct?

   A  The glass hotplate transfers less energy by radiation to the base of the pan than the metal
         hotplate.

   B  The glass hotplate transfers energy by convection to the base of the pan.

   C  The metal hotplate transfers energy by conduction to the base of the pan.

   D  The metal hotplate transfers energy by convection to the base of the pan.
```
