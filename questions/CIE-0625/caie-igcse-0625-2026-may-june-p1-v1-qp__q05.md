```text
5  The density of oil is 0.85 g / cm3. The density of pure water is 1.00 g / cm3. The density of mercury
     is 13.6 g / cm3.

   A metal object sinks in pure water and sinks in oil. The object floats in mercury.

   What is the density of the metal?

   A   greater than 13.6 g / cm3

   B   less than 13.6 g / cm3 but greater than 1.00 g / cm3

   C   less than 1.00 g / cm3 but greater than 0.85 g / cm3

   D   less than 0.85 g / cm3
```
