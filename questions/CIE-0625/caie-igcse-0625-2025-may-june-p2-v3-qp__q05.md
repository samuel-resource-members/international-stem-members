5  Samples of three different liquids have equal masses.

   The volumes of the samples are 190 cm3, 200 cm3 and 235 cm3.

   The liquids are poured into a large measuring cylinder and the liquids form three separate layers
    because they do not mix.

   What are the readings on the measuring cylinder at the two boundaries between the samples?

   A  190 cm3 and 390 cm3

   B  190 cm3 and 425 cm3

   C  235 cm3 and 425 cm3

   D  235 cm3 and 435 cm3
