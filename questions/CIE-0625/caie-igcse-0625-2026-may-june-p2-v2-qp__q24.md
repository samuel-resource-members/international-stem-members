24  In still air, the air pressure is p and the average distance between the air particles is d.

   A sound wave produces rarefactions as it travels through the air.

     In a rarefaction, the air pressure is P and the average distance between the air particles is D.

   Which statement is correct?

   A  P > p and D > d

   B  P > p and D < d

   C  P < p and D > d

   D  P < p and D < d
