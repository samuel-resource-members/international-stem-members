31 A relay consists of a coil and it is a type of switch. It is turned on and off using an electromagnet.

      Four statements explaining how the relay works are listed.

             1  An electromagnetic field forms around the coil.

             2   Contacts are pushed together, completing the circuit.

             3   There is a current in the coil.

             4   This attracts a soft-iron armature.

     What is the correct order for the statements that explains how the relay works?
