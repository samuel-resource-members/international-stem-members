```text
13 A glass beaker contains a mass of 0.20 kg of water at a temperature of 20 °C.

   The specific heat capacity of water is 4200 J / (kg °C).

   A brass block of mass 0.15 kg is at a temperature of 170 °C. The block is carefully lowered into
    the beaker of water.

   The final temperature of both the water and brass is 30 °C.

   What is the specific heat capacity of the brass?

   (Assume that no water is lost and no thermal energy has been transferred to the beaker or the
    surroundings.)

   A  400 J / (kg °C)

   B  660 J / (kg °C)

   C  1200 J / (kg °C)

   D  5600 J / (kg °C)
```
