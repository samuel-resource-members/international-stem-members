## Text (OCR)

```text
A circuit consists of an a.c. supply and two lamps. The lamps are connected in parallel.
(b) The electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the a.c. supply is 230 V. When connected to the 230 V 
supply, the resistance of one lamp is 1200 Ω and the resistance of the other lamp is 800 Ω.
 
(i) Calculate the current in the 800 Ω lamp.
 
(ii) Calculate the energy used by the 800 Ω lamp in 5.0 hours.
 
 Give your answer in kWh.
 
(iii) Calculate the combined resistance of the two lamps in this circuit.
```
