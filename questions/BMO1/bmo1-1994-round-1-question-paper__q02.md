2. In triangle ABC the point X lies on BC.
    (i) Suppose that BAC = 90◦, that X is the midpoint of BC,
      and that BAX is one third of BAC. What can you say
      (and prove!) about triangle ACX?
   (ii)Suppose that BAC = 60◦, that X lies one third of the
     way from B to C, and that AX bisects BAC. What can
      you say (and prove!) about triangle ACX?
