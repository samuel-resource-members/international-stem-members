Round 1: Thursday, 4 December 2008

1. Consider a standard 8 × 8 chessboard consisting of 64 small squares
   coloured in the usual pattern, so 32 are black and 32 are white. A
   zig-zag path across the board is a collection of eight white squares,
   one in each row, which meet at their corners. How many zig-zag paths
