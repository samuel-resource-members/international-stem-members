## Text (OCR)

```text
1.  Starting with any three digit number n (such as n = 625) we
   obtain a new number f(n) which is equal to the sum of the
   three digits of n, their three products in pairs, and the product
    of all three digits.
    (i) Find the value of n/f(n) when n = 625. (The answer is
      an integer!)
   (ii)Find all three digit numbers such that the ratio n/f(n)=1.
```
