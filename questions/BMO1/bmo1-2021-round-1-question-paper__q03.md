3. A square piece of paper is folded in half along a line of symmetry. The
   resulting shape is then folded in half along a line of symmetry of the new
   shape. This process is repeated until 𝑛folds have been made, giving a
   sequence of 𝑛+ 1 shapes. If we do not distinguish between congruent shapes,
   find the number of possible sequences when:
    (a) 𝑛= 3;
    (b) 𝑛= 6;
    (c) 𝑛= 9.
  (When 𝑛= 1 there are two possible sequences.)
