## Text (OCR)

```text
1. Consider the pair of four-digit positive integers
                 (M, N) = (3600, 2500).
   Notice that M and N are both perfect squares, with equal
    digits in two places, and diﬀering digits in the remaining
   two places. Moreover, when the digits diﬀer, the digit in M
    is exactly one greater than the corresponding digit in N.
   Find all pairs of four-digit positive integers (M, N) with these
   properties.
```
