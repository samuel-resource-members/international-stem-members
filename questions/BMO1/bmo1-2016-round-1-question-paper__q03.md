```text
3. Suppose that a sequence t0, t1, t2, . . . is deﬁned by a formula tn =
  An2 + Bn + C for all integers n ≥0.  Here A, B and C are real
   constants with A ̸= 0. Determine values of A, B and C which give the
   greatest possible number of successive terms of the sequence which
   are also successive terms of the Fibonacci sequence. The Fibonacci
   sequence is deﬁned by F0 = 0, F1 = 1 and Fm = Fm−1 + Fm−2 for
  m ≥2.
```
