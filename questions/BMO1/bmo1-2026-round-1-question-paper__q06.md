## Text (OCR)

```text
6. There are 1000 lily pads on a pond arranged in a circle and labelled
   1, 2, . . . , 1000 in order. (The first four lily pads may also be described using
   the labels 1001, 1002, 1003 and 1004, respectively.) The first 𝑛lily pads are
  each occupied by a frog with the remaining lily pads not occupied.

  Each minute, exactly one of the frogs makes a move. Suppose the frog is on
   lily pad 𝑘. That frog may either:
    (i) Swim to lily pad 𝑘+ 4 or 𝑘−4, provided that it is not occupied; or
    (ii) Jump to lily pad 𝑘+ 3 or 𝑘−3 provided that this lily pad is not occupied
  and the two lily pads jumped over are both occupied. When this happens,
   the two frogs that were jumped over dive into the pond and don’t participate
   in any further moves.
  For which values of 𝑛is it possible, by a sequence of moves, to end with
   exactly one frog remaining on the lily pads?
```
