```text
2.   Two balls, P and Q, are projected from the edge of a cliff. P is projected horizontally at speed v and
   Q is projected vertically upwards at the same speed. The time taken for P to reach the ground is t P
     and the speed at impact with the ground is vP . Air resistance is negligible.

    What is the time taken to reach the ground and the speed at impact with the ground for Q?

                Time taken           Speed at impact
     A.                               t P                           vP
     B.                               t P                   Greater than vP
     C.          Greater than t P                     vP
     D.          Greater than t P             Greater than vP
```
