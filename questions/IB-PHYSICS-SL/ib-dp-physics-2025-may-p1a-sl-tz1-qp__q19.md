19.  Charge is moving in a wire that is at right angles to a uniform magnetic field.

    The length of the wire is 0.32 m.

    When the current in the wire is increased by 5.0 A, the force acting on the wire increases
     by 4.0 mN.

    What is the strength of the magnetic field?

     B.   25 mT

     C.   40 T

     D.   40 kT
