## Text (OCR)

```text
Water is heated from room temperature to its boiling point in a metal cooking pot.

Plan an experiment to investigate if the time taken for water to reach its boiling point depends on
the metal from which the cooking pot is made.

You are provided with:

•   a supply of cold water
•   a set of three cooking pots of the same size, one made of aluminium, one made of copper
    and one made of steel
•   a Bunsen burner, a tripod and gauze
•   a measuring cylinder.

You may use any other common laboratory apparatus.

In your plan include:

•   any other apparatus needed
•   a brief description of the method, including what you will measure and how you will make
    sure your measurements are accurate
•    the variables you will control
•   a results table to record the measurements (you do not need to make any measurements or
    record any results in the table)
•   how you will process your results to draw a conclusion.

You may include a labelled diagram if you wish.
```
