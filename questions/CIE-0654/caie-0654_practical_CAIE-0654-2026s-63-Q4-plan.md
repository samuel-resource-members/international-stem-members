## Text (OCR)

```text
When manganese(IV) oxide powder is added to aqueous hydrogen peroxide, the
hydrogen peroxide decomposes and forms oxygen gas.

                       hydrogen peroxide   oxygen + water

Plan an investigation to determine the relationship between the rate of this reaction and the initial
temperature of the aqueous hydrogen peroxide.

You are provided with an unlimited supply of:

•   aqueous hydrogen peroxide
•   manganese(IV) oxide powder.

You may use any common laboratory apparatus.

In your plan, include:

•    the apparatus you will need
•   a brief description of the method
•    the measurements you will make, including how you will make them as accurate as possible
•    the variables you will control
•   how you will use your results to reach a conclusion.

You may include a labelled diagram in your answer.

You may include a results table in your answer. You are not required to enter any readings in
the table.
```
