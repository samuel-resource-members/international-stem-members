A student investigates how the current in a series circuit changes with the number of lamps
connected in the circuit.

Plan an investigation to determine the relationship between the current in a series circuit and the
number of lamps connected in the circuit.

You are provided with:

•   a number of identical lamps
•   connecting wires.

You may use any common laboratory apparatus in your plan.

In your plan, include:

•    the apparatus you will use
•   a brief description of the method
•    the variables you will control and how you will ensure your results are valid
•   a results table for your measurements (you are not required to enter any readings into
    the table)
•   how you will process your results to form a conclusion.
