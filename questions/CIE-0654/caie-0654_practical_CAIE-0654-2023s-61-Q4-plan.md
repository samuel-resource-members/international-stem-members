## Text (OCR)

```text
Aqueous hydrogen peroxide is a solution that breaks down slowly into water and oxygen gas.

                       hydrogen peroxide    water + oxygen

A catalyst is a substance that increases the rate of a chemical reaction.

Manganese(IV) oxide is a catalyst for this reaction.

The reaction is too fast to count the bubbles of gas formed.

Plan an investigation to find the relationship between the mass of manganese(IV) oxide added
and the rate of this reaction.

You are provided with:

•   aqueous hydrogen peroxide
•   manganese(IV) oxide solid.

You may use any common laboratory apparatus.

Include in your plan:

•    the apparatus needed
•   a brief description of the method, with an explanation of any safety precautions
•    the measurements you will make, including how to make them as accurate as possible
•    the variables you will control
•   how you will use your results to draw a conclusion.

You may include a diagram if it helps to explain your plan.

You may include a results table. You are not required to include any results.
```
