## Text (OCR)

```text
A ball is dropped from rest at a height H above the ground. The ball rebounds and bounces back
up to a height h.

Plan an experiment to investigate the relationship between the bounce height h and the height H
from which the ball is dropped.

You are provided with a selection of balls of different sizes and different materials.

You may use any other apparatus normally found in a school laboratory.

Include in your answer:

•    the apparatus you will use

•   a brief description of the method, including how you will ensure your results are accurate

•    the variables you will control

•   a table with column headings to show how you will present your results (you are not required
     to enter any readings in the table)

•   how you will process your results to reach a conclusion.

You may include a labelled diagram if it helps to explain your plan.
```
