```text
2.  The organizer for a sports league with four teams has entered some of the end-of-season
    data into the table shown. Each team played 27 games and each game resulted in a win
     for one team and a loss for the other team, or in a tie for both teams. Each team earned
    2 points for a win, 0 points for a loss, and 1 point for a tie.

           Team  Games  Number  Number  Number   Total
          Name  Played   of Wins   of Losses   of Ties   Points
             P       27        10         14                  23
           Q       27
           R       27                                       25
              S       27

          (a) How many ties did Team P have at the end of the season?

         (b) Team Q had 2 more wins than Team P and 4 fewer losses than Team P. How
          many total points did Team Q have at the end of the season?

          (c) Explain why Team R could not have ﬁnished the season with exactly 6 ties.

         (d) At the end of the season, Team S had 4 more wins than losses. Show that Team S
           must have ﬁnished the season with a total of 31 points.
```
