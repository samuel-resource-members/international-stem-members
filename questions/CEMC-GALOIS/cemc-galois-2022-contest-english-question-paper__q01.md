## Text (OCR)

```text
1.     Alice and Bello contributed to the cost of starting a new business. The ratio of Alice’s
      contribution to Bello’s contribution was 3 : 8.

          (a) If the cost of starting the new business was $9240, what was Bello’s contribution
             to this starting cost?

         (b) Alice and Bello divided up all proﬁts in the ﬁrst year of the business in the same
               ratio, 3 : 8. Alice’s share of the ﬁrst year’s total proﬁt was $1881. What was the
              total proﬁt of the business for the ﬁrst year?

          (c) In the second year, the business was changed so the share of that year’s proﬁts
              for Alice and Bello was in the ratio of 3 : (8+x). If the proﬁt for the second year
           was $6400 and Bello’s share of that proﬁt was $5440, determine the value of x.
```
