3.  Rectangle ABCD has vertices A(0, 0), B(0, 12), C(6, 12), and D(6, 0).

          (a) Diagonals AC and BD intersect at point E. What is the area of △ADE?

         (b) Point P(0, p) lies on line segment AB. The area of trapezoid BCDP is twice the
            area of △PAD. What is the value of p?

          (c) The line passing through U(0, u), V (2, 4) and W(6, w) divides ABCD into two
             trapezoids. Determine all possible pairs of points U and W for which the ratio
              of the areas of these two trapezoids is 5 : 3.
