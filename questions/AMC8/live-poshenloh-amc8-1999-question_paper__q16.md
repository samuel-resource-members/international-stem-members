## Statement

Tori’s mathematics test had \(75\) problems: \(10\) arithmetic, \(30\) algebra, and \(35\) geometry problems. Although she answered \(70\%\) of the arithmetic, \(40\%\) of the algebra, and \(60\%\) of the geometry problems correctly, she did not pass the test because she got less than \(60\%\) of the problems right.

How many more problems would she have needed to answer correctly to earn a \(60\%\) passing grade?
