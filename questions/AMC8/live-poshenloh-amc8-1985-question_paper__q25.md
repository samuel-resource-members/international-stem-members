## Statement

Five cards are lying on a table. Each card has a letter on one side and a whole number on the other side. The visible faces show \(P,\) \(Q,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) and \(6.\) Jane said, “If a vowel is on one side of any card, then an even number is on the other side.” Mary showed Jane was wrong by turning over one card. Which card did Mary turn over?
