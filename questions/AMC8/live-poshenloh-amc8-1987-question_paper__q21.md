## Statement

Suppose \(n^\ast\) means \(\dfrac1n,\) the reciprocal of \(n.\) For example, \(5^\ast = \dfrac15.\) How many of the following four statements are true?

\[\mathrm{(i)}\quad 3^\ast + 6^\ast = 9^\ast\]

\[\mathrm{(ii)}\quad 6^\ast - 4^\ast = 2^\ast\]

\[\mathrm{(iii)}\quad 2^\ast \cdot 6^\ast = 12^\ast\]

\[\mathrm{(iv)}\quad 10^\ast \div 2^\ast = 5^\ast\]
