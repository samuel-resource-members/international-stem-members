3. Wind chill is a measure of how cold people feel when exposed to wind outside. A good
   estimate for wind chill can be found using this calculation:

               W = T −0.7 ⋅S

                           ∘   degrees Fahrenheit ( F), and S represents wind speed measured in miles per hour

                                          ∘   Suppose the air temperature is 36 F and the wind speed is 18 mph. Which of the

     A   18

    C   28

    D   32
