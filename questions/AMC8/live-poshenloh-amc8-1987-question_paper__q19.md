## Statement

A calculator has a squaring key \(\boxed{x^2}\) that replaces the number currently displayed with its square. For example, if the display reads \(\boxed{\phantom{00}3}\) and \(\boxed{x^2}\) is pressed, the display becomes \(\boxed{\phantom{00}9}.\) If the display reads \(\boxed{\phantom{00}2},\) how many times must \(\boxed{x^2}\) be pressed to produce a displayed number greater than \(500?\)
