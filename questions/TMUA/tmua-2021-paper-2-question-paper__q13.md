13  A region R in the (x, y)-plane is deﬁned by the simultaneous inequalities

                                        y −x < 3

                                       y −x2 < 1


     Which of the following statements is/are true for every point in R ?


             I   −1 < x < 2

             II   (y −x)(y −x2) < 3

            III  y < 5

   A  none of them

   B  I only

   C  II only

   D  III only

    E   I and II only

    F   I and III only

   G  II and III only

   H   I, II and III
