```text
16   In this question, x1, x2, x3, . . . is an arithmetic progression, all of whose terms
      are integers.

      Let n be a positive integer. If the median of the ﬁrst n terms of the sequence is an
       integer, which of the following three statements must be true?


             I   The median of the ﬁrst n + 2 terms is an integer.

             II  The median of the ﬁrst 2n terms is an integer.

            III  The median of x2, x4, x6, . . . , x2n is an integer.


   A  none of them

   B  I only

   C  II only

   D  III only

    E   I and II only

    F   I and III only

   G  II and III only

   H   I, II and III
```
