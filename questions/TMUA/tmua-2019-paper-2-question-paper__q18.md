## Text (OCR)

```text
18   Consider the following inequality:


             (∗):    a|x| + 1 ≤|x −2|


     where a is a real constant.

     Which one of the following describes the complete set of values of a such that (∗) is
     true for all real x?


   A  a ≤3                  2

   B  a ≤1

   C  a ≤1                  2

   D  a ≤0

    E  a ≤−1                    2

    F  a ≤−1

   G  a ≤−3                    2

   H  There are no such values of a.
```
