8   A student draws a triangle that is acute-angled or obtuse-angled but not right-angled.

    The student counts the number of straight lines that divide the triangle into two triangles, at
      least one of which is right-angled.

     Which of the following statements is/are true?

              I   The student can draw a triangle for which there is exactly 1 such straight line.

              II   The student can draw a triangle for which there are exactly 2 such straight lines.

              III  The student can draw a triangle for which there are exactly 3 such straight lines.


    A  none of them

    B   I only

    C   II only

    D   III only

    E   I and II only

    F    I and III only

   G   II and III only
