11   Consider the equation 2x = mx + c, where m and c are real constants.

     Which of the following statements is/are true?


             I   The equation has a negative real solution only if c > 1.

             II  The equation has two distinct real solutions if c > 1.

            III  The equation has two distinct positive real solutions if and only if c ≤1.


   A  none of them

   B  I only

   C  II only

   D  III only

    E   I and II only

    F   I and III only

   G  II and III only

   H   I, II and III
