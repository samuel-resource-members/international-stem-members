MEMES
Richard Dawkins (1941-present) invented the idea of memes, and Sir Frederic Bartlett (1886-1969) showed
previous knowledge alters the processing of new stimuli. Bartlett asked British people to reproduce a drawing
of Native American masks from memory. This was passed to a new person to memorise and reproduce, and the
reproduction passed on, several times in succession (1). This study has been repeated recently (2).
Representative reproductions are shown.
