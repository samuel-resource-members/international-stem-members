## Text (OCR)

```text
Blood Types
Among 1290 individuals of a population under study, the numbers of individuals with blood group 
types M, MN, and N are, respectively, 340, 880, and 70. 
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

In a start generation with frequency of M = 0.6 and frequency of N = 0.4, after
three generations of random mating the frequency of MM individuals in the
population of the fourth generation who are offspring of MN*MN matings will
be <10%.
```
