```text
Aneuploidy
Electrophoresis patterns of PCR products of several chromosome pairs (pairs shown on bottom) in five 
different individual (1–5) in figure below allow evaluation of presence or absence of chromosome 
number abnormality. Monosomy of autosomal chromosomes is known to be lethal. Relative height of 
the peaks in each box reflects the copy number ratio of the two chromosomes in that box. The 
horizontal axis shows migration, and the vertical axis shows fluorescence intensity. 
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

PCR products related to different chromosomes should have different sizes to
allow copy number evaluation.
```
