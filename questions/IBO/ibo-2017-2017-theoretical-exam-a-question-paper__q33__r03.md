diabetes treatment. This inspired similar investigations into body weight homeostasis and obesity: two mouse

single, but different, gene. Damaging the arcuate nucleus in the brain, which is the structure soley responsible
for regulating the sensation hunger, produces a similar phenotype. Mice were joined surgically, allowing a
small amount of blood to flow between them, and then observed.
