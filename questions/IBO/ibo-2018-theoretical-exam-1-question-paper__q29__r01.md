```text
Nitrogen Fixation
Nitrogen is an important nutrient for all plants. While there is an abundance of nitrogen (N2) in the 
atmosphere, most plants are unable to convert N2 into a usable form. Fixation of nitrogen gas into 
ammonia is an ability restricted to nitrogen-fixing bacteria. The figure below shows prerequisite step 
for nitrogen fixation through symbiosis between these bacteria and some plant species. 
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

Between two major inorganic nitrogen forms, NH4+ and NO3−, the former is
mobile in the plant, while the latter is not.
```
