A MODEL OF HOME BLOOD GLUCOSE MEASUREMENT 在家中進⾏⾎糖測定
Glucose in the mammalian body is metabolized in a complex system which provides a balance (home-
ostasis) of highly regulated processes.
哺乳動物體內的葡萄糖以複雜的代謝系統運作以提供能進⾏⾼度調節的穩定平衡(homeostasis) 狀況。
Suﬃcient levels of blood glucose are maintained hormonally and allosterically by the regulation of trans-
porters and key enzymes of glucose metabolism.
通過賀爾蒙及酵素易位(allosterically) 調節葡萄糖運輸蛋⽩及葡萄糖代謝相關主要酵素，可維持⾜夠量的⾎
糖。
Metabolic diseases that result in a disturbance of these regulating mechanisms have severe consequences
to the aﬀected individuals and have become of economic signiﬁcance on a global level.
導致⾎糖調節機制紊亂的代謝疾病對受影響的個體產⽣嚴重後果，並對全球經濟造成重⼤影響。
The most common of them all ‒diabetes mellitus ‒is a group of metabolic diseases that have chronic hy-
perglycemia (elevated blood glucose levels) in common as a result of disturbances in insulin secretion
and/or the eﬀects of insulin.
其中最常⾒的- 糖尿病- 是⼀組代謝性疾病，起因於胰島素分泌紊亂和/或胰島素效應問題所造成的慢性⾼⾎糖
症（⾎糖量升⾼）。
For the management of diabetes, monitoring blood glucose levels is essential. Especially patients under-
going insulin therapy are required to measure their blood glucose levels on a daily basis, since the calcu-
lation of the correct doses of insulin is essential to avoid hypo- and hyperglycemia, both life-threatening
situations. Correct insulin dosage and consistently suﬃcient blood glucose levels are also key elements
for prognosis improvement.
對於糖尿病的管理，監測⾎糖量⾄關重要。特別是在接受胰島素治療的患者需要每天測量他們的⾎糖量，因為
正確劑量的胰島素的計算對於避免低⾎糖和⾼⾎糖都是必不可少，這兩種情況都是危及⽣命的。正確的胰島素
劑量和穩定⾜夠⾎糖量也是改善預後的關鍵因素。
In this practical exam, handheld glucose meters developed for home-use by diabetes patients will be used
to simulate blood glucose measurement in model glucose solutions.
在本實作中，將以糖尿病患者使⽤的家庭⽤⼿持式⾎糖儀進⾏葡萄糖溶液量測以模擬⾎糖量。
Glucometers are used for home blood glucose monitoring by patients with diabetes mellitus and other
diseases with a high risk for hypoglycemia.
⾎糖儀⽤於患有糖尿病和其他具有低⾎糖⾵險的疾病的患者家族的⾎糖監測。
The glucometer used for these determines blood glucose content with reﬂectance photometry using the
hexokinase method:
這些量測⾎糖的⾎糖儀使⽤⼰糖激酶(hexokinase) 法通過反射光度法測定⾎糖含量：
Since glucometers are calibrated using whole blood, calibration curves have to be prepared to obtain cor-
rect measurement results when using aqueous solutions.
由於⾎糖儀是使⽤全⾎校準的，因此在使⽤⽔溶液時必須準備校準曲線以獲得正確的測量結果。

Dependence of Blood Glucose Measurement on Sample Temperature ⾎糖測量對樣本溫度的依賴性
Glucose concentration values measured by some glucometers are aﬀected by temperature. To conﬁrm or
refute this, you will measure the glucose concentration of the solution previously diluted 1:2 at diﬀerent
temperatures.

Procedure 實驗步驟
1. Label 4 Eppendorf tubes with the respective temperature. You are going to measure the glucose

competitor´s ID number legibly. 將4 個微量離⼼管標記好各種溫度。您將在0°C，10°C，室溫（RT）
和37°C 下測量葡萄糖濃度。在37°C 的管⼦上，還要清楚地加標上參賽者的編號。
