In this practical your task will be to study and draw several types of starch grains.

(A: bean, B: wheat grain, C: potato, D: rice grain) Write the appropriate letter (A, B, C or D) on the slide with

2. Drip one drop of Lugol’s solution (iodine solution with KI) on the scrapings, cover it with a cover slip
and examine them at a 100x total magniﬁcation under a microscope.
在刮取的組織上滴⼀滴魯哥爾溶液（含KI 的碘溶液），蓋上蓋玻⽚，在顯微鏡下以100 倍之放⼤倍率檢查。

lution and call the lab supervisors to prove your work.
製備好所有組織切⽚後置於顯微鏡下觀察，得到清晰的影像後，告知實驗監督⼈員

Be aware that you need these slides for the next tasks. 請注意，你需要這些組織
切⽚以完成下⼀題。
