CUCKOOS
The Island of Great Britain is a critical hub for migrating birds, from as far away as Canada and South Africa,
due to its mild climate and well preserved habitats.
Cuckoos (Cuculus canorus) are one migratory species which has seen its population decline in recent years.

migration routes are shown (1). A roughly equal number of birds took the Eastern and Western routes through
Africa. Points at which tracked birds died are shown.
