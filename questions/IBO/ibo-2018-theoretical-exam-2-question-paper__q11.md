Enzyme - Coenzyme Interaction

the enzymes with their coenzyme, which in turn lowers the rate of the reaction. The cofactor of a 
group of enzymes called TPP- or thiamine-dependent enzymes such as transketolase is thiamine 
pyrophosphate (TPP).

individual’s tissue were compared under condition of substrate saturation. The TPP-free forms of the 
enzyme was prepared and used for enzyme kinetic measurements. The Lineweaver-Burk like plots are 
shown below.

Maximum velocity of B is higher than the maximum velocity of A.
It can be concluded that the enzyme in A has a lower affinity for its substrate(s).
Administration of thiamine to patients is expected to restore enzymatic activity.
