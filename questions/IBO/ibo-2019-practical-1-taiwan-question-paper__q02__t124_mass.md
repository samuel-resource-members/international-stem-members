## Text (OCR)

```text
Assume that the average volume of a starch grain is 4000 (𝜇𝑚)3 . The starch
grain can be considered a 20% aqueous solution of starch with a density of 1.050
g/𝑐𝑚3. What is the weight of the starch constituting this one grain? Give you
answer in mm rounded to the nearest integer (1 𝜇𝑚= 10−6 𝑚).
假設澱粉粒的平均體積是4000 μm3 。澱粉粒可視為是20％，密度為1.050 g/cm3
的澱粉⽔溶液。構成這⼀粒澱粉的重量是多少？以mm 為單位，並以最接近的整數
寫出你的答案。
```
