```text
ANCIENT DNA
Mark Thomas (1964-present) read the first DNA sequences from woolly mammoths (Mammuthus
primigenius). However, ancient DNA analysis remains difficult, due to degradation, contamination, and
polymerase inhibitors found in the samples. Scientists attempted to extract DNA from a dinosaur fossil and
analyse the sequence of one specific gene. The following phylogeny was produced after comparing
the sequence against other species.
Humans
Dinosaurs
Bats
Dogs
Rodents
Whales
Cows
Cuckoos
Frogs

True False

It is better to sequence very long DNA fragments from ancient samples, than very short fragments.
```
