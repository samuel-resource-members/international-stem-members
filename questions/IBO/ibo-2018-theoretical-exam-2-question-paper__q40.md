```text
Sieve-Tubes
Plants conduct sugars and other substances throughout their body by means of phloem. Phloem is a 
compound tissue, including different cell types (e.g. sieve-tubes elements (in angiosperms), 
companion cells and parenchymal cells.). In a study, DNA genomic samples prepared from sieve-tubes 
and companion cells of an angiosperm and investigated for DNA corresponding to X. Gene-specific 
primers were used to amplify gene X among whole genome of cells by PCR. 
Note: Each band represents a unique DNA molecule, asterisks denotes bands corresponding to 
functional genes. 
Results of sieve-tube element genome analysis suggest that the plant is
heterozygote in the gene X.
The results obtained for the sieve-tube cells can be explained by gene X being
present both in the mitochondrial and chloroplast DNA.
Band A most likely corresponds to an organelle-related DNA sequences in the
nuclear genome.
Band B most likely corresponds to a pseudogene in nuclear genome.
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:
```
