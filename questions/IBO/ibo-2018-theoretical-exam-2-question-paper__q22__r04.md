## Text (OCR)

```text
Coombs Test
There are two clinical tests to detect some antibodies against erythrocytes (RBCs), direct coombs test 
and indirect coombs test. 
During the direct coombs test a blood sample is taken from a person. RBCs are washed (removing the 
patient’s own plasma) and then incubated with anti-human globulin, which attaches to all IgG 
antibodies. Coombs test is positive if agglutination reaction occurs. 
In the indirect coombs test, serum is extracted from the blood sample taken from the person. Then, the 
serum gets incubated with RBCs of known antigenicity and is washed. Finally, anti-human globulin is 
added. If agglutination occurs, the indirect coombs test is positive. 
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

In a positive indirect coombs test, antigen-antibody reaction happens in vivo.
```
