## Text (OCR)

```text
Endothermy
Endotherm animals can only tolerate narrow range of body temperature (30–45 ºC), but their ability to 
generate heat internally allowed them to expand their distributions. On the other hand, the cost to be 
endotherm is high because of their constant demand for food to support the energy needed for heat 
production. 
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

Basal metabolic rate of an endotherm animal stays constant throughout a
limited range of environmental temperatures.
```
