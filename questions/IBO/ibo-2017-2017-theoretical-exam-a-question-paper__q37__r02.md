WINDFARMS
The UK is extending the world’s largest offshore windfarm in the North Sea. However, disruption to wind
patterns, and other effects, causes difficulties for birds. Several windfarms were previously built in the habitats
of red grouse (Lagopus lagopus scotica), snipe (Gallinago gallinago), skylark (Alauda arvensis), curlew
(Numenius sp.), and meadow pipit (Anthus pratensis). The population density of these species was compared to
undisturbed habitats before, during and after windfarm construction.
