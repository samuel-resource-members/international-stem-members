through the medium in search for food, while sedentary sitters do not. It seems that these foraging
strategies is genetically determined.

population for five consecutive generations (Gen1 to Gen5).
Using the film from the fruit flies larva, complete the table below (Use the same method as part A to
calculate H; Correct to two digits after decimal point).
Note: For a larva to be counted as a sitter, its whole body should stay in or  touch the boundary  of a
