## Text (OCR)

```text
Restriction Enzyme Digestion
Some restriction enzymes have different recognition sequences but create the same sticky ends, as 
shown below for SalI and XhoI. 
The following gel electrophoresis image shows linearized DNAs obtained after complete restriction 
enzyme digestion of non-recombinant (empty vector) and recombinant (containing gene X) expression 
plasmid vectors. The expression vector includes a strong promoter near its cloning site. The insert of 
the recombinant plasmid was a product of SalI digestion. The insert was ligated into the vector which 
had been cut with XhoI. 
* All recombinant vectors gave the same pattern in the gel
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

The data indicate that two copies of the insert were cloned in the expression
vector.
```
