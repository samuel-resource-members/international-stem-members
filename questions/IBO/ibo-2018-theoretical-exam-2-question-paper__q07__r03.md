## Text (OCR)

```text
Blood Types
Among 1290 individuals of a population under study, the numbers of individuals with blood group 
types M, MN, and N are, respectively, 340, 880, and 70. 
Indicate if each of the following statements is true or false:

If there is random mating in a population in which frequency of M alleles is 0.6
and frequency of N alleles is 0.4, the frequency of offspring with the NN
genotype will be 0.16.
```
