A MODEL OF HOME BLOOD GLUCOSE MEASUREMENT 在家中進⾏⾎糖測定
Glucose in the mammalian body is metabolized in a complex system which provides a balance (home-
ostasis) of highly regulated processes.
哺乳動物體內的葡萄糖以複雜的代謝系統運作以提供能進⾏⾼度調節的穩定平衡(homeostasis) 狀況。
Suﬃcient levels of blood glucose are maintained hormonally and allosterically by the regulation of trans-
porters and key enzymes of glucose metabolism.
通過賀爾蒙及酵素易位(allosterically) 調節葡萄糖運輸蛋⽩及葡萄糖代謝相關主要酵素，可維持⾜夠量的⾎
糖。
Metabolic diseases that result in a disturbance of these regulating mechanisms have severe consequences
to the aﬀected individuals and have become of economic signiﬁcance on a global level.
導致⾎糖調節機制紊亂的代謝疾病對受影響的個體產⽣嚴重後果，並對全球經濟造成重⼤影響。
The most common of them all ‒diabetes mellitus ‒is a group of metabolic diseases that have chronic hy-
perglycemia (elevated blood glucose levels) in common as a result of disturbances in insulin secretion
and/or the eﬀects of insulin.
其中最常⾒的- 糖尿病- 是⼀組代謝性疾病，起因於胰島素分泌紊亂和/或胰島素效應問題所造成的慢性⾼⾎糖
症（⾎糖量升⾼）。
For the management of diabetes, monitoring blood glucose levels is essential. Especially patients under-
going insulin therapy are required to measure their blood glucose levels on a daily basis, since the calcu-
lation of the correct doses of insulin is essential to avoid hypo- and hyperglycemia, both life-threatening
situations. Correct insulin dosage and consistently suﬃcient blood glucose levels are also key elements
for prognosis improvement.
對於糖尿病的管理，監測⾎糖量⾄關重要。特別是在接受胰島素治療的患者需要每天測量他們的⾎糖量，因為
正確劑量的胰島素的計算對於避免低⾎糖和⾼⾎糖都是必不可少，這兩種情況都是危及⽣命的。正確的胰島素
劑量和穩定⾜夠⾎糖量也是改善預後的關鍵因素。
In this practical exam, handheld glucose meters developed for home-use by diabetes patients will be used
to simulate blood glucose measurement in model glucose solutions.
在本實作中，將以糖尿病患者使⽤的家庭⽤⼿持式⾎糖儀進⾏葡萄糖溶液量測以模擬⾎糖量。
Glucometers are used for home blood glucose monitoring by patients with diabetes mellitus and other
diseases with a high risk for hypoglycemia.
⾎糖儀⽤於患有糖尿病和其他具有低⾎糖⾵險的疾病的患者家族的⾎糖監測。
The glucometer used for these determines blood glucose content with reﬂectance photometry using the
hexokinase method:
這些量測⾎糖的⾎糖儀使⽤⼰糖激酶(hexokinase) 法通過反射光度法測定⾎糖含量：
Since glucometers are calibrated using whole blood, calibration curves have to be prepared to obtain cor-
rect measurement results when using aqueous solutions.
由於⾎糖儀是使⽤全⾎校準的，因此在使⽤⽔溶液時必須準備校準曲線以獲得正確的測量結果。

How to Measure Glucose Concentrations with the ACCU-CHEK Active Blood Glucose Meter (see ﬁgure
below) 如何使⽤ACCU-CHEK 活性⾎糖儀測量葡萄糖濃度（⾒下圖）
1. Take a test strip from the test strip container. Close the container immediately. 從試紙容器中取出試
紙。⽴即關閉容器。
2. Hold the test strip so that the arrows printed on it and the green square face upwards 握住試紙，使
印有箭頭，及綠⾊正⽅形的⼀端朝上
3. Slide the test strip into the test strip guide in the direction of the arrows until you feel it lock into
place. (1) Do not bend the test strip. 沿箭頭⽅向將試紙滑⼊試紙槽上的箭頭⽅向，直到感覺到它鎖定到
位。備註（1）不要彎曲試紙。
4. The test strip must lie ﬂat on the measurement window cover. 試紙必須平放在測量窗蓋上。
5. The meter turns on and ﬁrst performs a default display check (approx. 2 seconds). (2) 將⾎糖儀打開
並進⾏預設顯⽰檢測（約2 秒）(2)。
6. Following the display check, the test strip symbol and the ﬂashing blood drop symbol appear on the
display (3). 在顯⽰檢測完成後，試紙符號和閃爍的⾎滴符號會呈現在顯⽰器上（3）。
7. The meter is now ready to perform a blood glucose test. You have approx. 90 seconds to apply 3 µL
of glucose solution onto the middle of the green square of the test strip. After this time, the meter
turns itself oﬀ. If this happens, remove the strip and slide it in again. ⾎糖儀現在已準備好進⾏⾎糖測
試。你有⼤約90 秒時間可將3μL 葡萄糖溶液滴加到試紙的綠⾊正⽅形的中間。超過此時間後，儀器會⾃
動關閉。如果發⽣這種情況，請取下試紙並重新再將之滑⼊。
8. The test starts. The ﬂashing hourglass symbol indicates that the test is in progress. 測試開始。閃爍
的沙漏符號表⽰測試正在進⾏中。
9. The test is over in approximately 5 seconds. 測試在⼤約5 秒內結束。
10. You can now mark the test result 您現在可以記錄測試結果。
11. After removing the test strip, the device will turn itself oﬀ. Discard any used test strips.
在移除試紙後，儀器將⾃⾏關閉。丟棄任何⽤過的試紙。
(If an error appears before the application of the liquid, remove the test strip and try again. If it doesn´t
work the second time or you already applied the glucose solution, discard the test strip and use a new
one.)
(如果在加⼊液體前出現錯誤訊號，請取下試紙並再試⼀次。如果第⼆次仍出現錯誤，或您已將葡萄糖溶液加
⼊，則丟棄試紙並使⽤另⼀新的試紙。)

Glucometer Measurement of the Concentration of Model Glucose Solutions 第1 部分。⾎糖儀
測量模型葡萄糖溶液的濃度
First, you are going to be testing the accuracy of glucose measurement with the glucose meter on water-
based model glucose solutions.
⾸先，您將使⽤⾎糖儀以測試葡萄糖⽔溶液中葡萄糖含量為模式，並測試⾎糖儀的準確性。
Procedure 程序
1. Prepare the following dilutions from the provided standard glucose solution in Eppendorf tubes us-
ing distilled water: 1:5; 1:2; 1:1; 2:1 (in a sample volume : diluent volume format) ‒300 µL of each.
Label the tubes accordingly. 使⽤蒸餾⽔，從提供的標準葡萄糖溶液中在微量離⼼管中製備以下稀釋液：
1：5；1：2；1：1；2：1（樣品體積：稀釋劑體積）- 每種300μL。在相應地離⼼管上標記。
2. Measure the glucose content of the solutions. 測量溶液的葡萄糖含量。

Dependence of Blood Glucose Measurement on Sample Temperature ⾎糖測量對樣本溫度的依賴性
Glucose concentration values measured by some glucometers are aﬀected by temperature. To conﬁrm or
refute this, you will measure the glucose concentration of the solution previously diluted 1:2 at diﬀerent
temperatures.
有些⾎糖儀測量的葡萄糖濃度值會受溫度影響。為了確認或反駁這⼀點，您將測量在不同溫度下以1：2 稀釋
的葡萄糖溶液的濃度。
Procedure 實驗步驟
1. Label 4 Eppendorf tubes with the respective temperature. You are going to measure the glucose
concentrations at 0 °C, 10 °C, room temperature (RT) and 37 °C. On the tube for 37 °C, also add your
competitor´s ID number legibly. 將4 個微量離⼼管標記好各種溫度。您將在0°C，10°C，室溫（RT）
和37°C 下測量葡萄糖濃度。在37°C 的管⼦上，還要清楚地加標上參賽者的編號。
2. To each tube, add 50 µL of the 1:2 diluted glucose solution. 向每個管中各加⼊50μL 的1：2 稀釋的葡
萄糖溶液。
3. Move the solutions to the respective temperatures: 0 °C in ice water, 10-15 °C by cooling room tem-
perature water with ice and controlling the temperature with a thermometer, room temperature left
on the rack and 37 °C in the dry thermostat. Leave the solutions to equilibrate for at least 5 minutes.
You´ll have to pay attention to keep the 10-15 °C beaker at the right temperature range. 將溶液移⾄
各⾃的溫度：冰⽔0°C，將室溫⽔⽤冰冷卻⾄10-15°C，⽤溫度計控制溫度，將室溫管⼦置於管架上，乾
浴槽溫度設為37°C。讓溶液平衡⾄少5 分鐘。您必須注意將10-15°C 的燒杯保持在合適的溫度範圍內。
4. After at least 5 minutes, measure the glucose concentrations with the glucometer. You have to work
quickly at this point, not to let the samples diﬀer too much from the required temperature. ⾄少5 分
鐘後，⽤⾎糖儀測量葡萄糖濃度。此時您必須快速⼯作，不要讓樣品與所需溫度相差太⼤。

Record the results of your measurements in the table on the answer sheet.
在答題紙上的表格中記錄您的測量結果。
Table Legend: 表格說明：
T ‒solution temperature RT ‒room temperature MC ‒Measured glucose concen-
tration, one decimal place accuracy T - 溶液溫度RT - 室溫MC - 測量的葡萄糖濃
度，⼩數點後⼀位精準度
