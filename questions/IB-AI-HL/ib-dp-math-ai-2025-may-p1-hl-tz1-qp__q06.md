Walkea Tree is a furniture store. They offer a  30 %  discount on all chairs priced over
250 Canadian dollars  (CAD) .
A different furniture store, LuxeCraft, offer customers a reduction of  150 CAD  on all chairs
priced over  250 CAD .
Given that  x  is the original price of a chair and  x > 250 ,
(a)
write down a function  f (x)  that models the final price of a chair at Walkea Tree,
(i)
after the discount is applied.
write down a function  g (x)  that models the final price of a chair at LuxeCraft,
(ii)
after the reduction is applied.
[2]
A third store, Opulent Furnishings, decides to offer both a  30 %  discount and a reduction
of  150 CAD  on all their chairs priced over  250 CAD .
Describe the meaning of  (  f  g) (x) , in context.
(b)
[2]
State, with justification, whether  (  f  g) (x)  or  ( g  f  ) (x)  is better for the customer
(c)
when they buy a chair with an original price of  450 CAD .
