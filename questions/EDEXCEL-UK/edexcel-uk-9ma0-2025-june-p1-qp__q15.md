## Statement

15. Figure 4 shows the plan view for the design of a stage.
The shape of this design consists of a sector of a circle AOB joined to a rectangle OBCD.
Given that
• the radius of the sector is r metres and angle AOB is θ radians
• the length and width of the rectangle are r metres and (1/10)r metres respectively
• the total area of the stage is 240 m²
(a) show that the perimeter of the stage, P metres, is given by
\[P=2r+\frac{480}{r}.\]
You must make your method clear. (4)
Using algebraic differentiation,
(b) find the value of r for which P has a stationary value, (3)
(c) Prove, by further differentiation, that this value of r gives the minimum perimeter of the stage. (2)
