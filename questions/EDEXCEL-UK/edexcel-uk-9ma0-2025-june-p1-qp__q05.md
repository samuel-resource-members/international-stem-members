## Statement

5. (a) Express \(\displaystyle\lim_{\delta x\to0}\sum_{x=1.44}^{2.89}\frac{2}{\sqrt{x}}\,\delta x\) as an integral. (1)
(b) Hence show that
\[\lim_{\delta x\to0}\sum_{x=1.44}^{2.89}\frac{2}{\sqrt{x}}\,\delta x=k\]
where k is an integer to be found.
(Solutions relying on calculator technology are not acceptable.) (2)
