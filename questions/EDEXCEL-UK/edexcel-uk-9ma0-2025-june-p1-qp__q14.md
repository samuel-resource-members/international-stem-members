## Statement

14. In this question you must show all stages of your working.
Solutions relying on calculator technology are not acceptable.
(a) Show that
\[\sin(x+30^\circ)+\sqrt3\cos(x+30^\circ)\equiv2\cos x.\] (3)
(b) Hence solve, for 0 ≤ θ < 180°,
\[\sin(\theta+30^\circ)+\sqrt3\cos(\theta+30^\circ)=3\sin2\theta\]
giving your answers to one decimal place, where appropriate. (4)
