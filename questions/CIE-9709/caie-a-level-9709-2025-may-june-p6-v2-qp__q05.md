5   The amount of time, in minutes, spent by a customer on one visit to a certain shop is modelled by the
    random variable X + N ( n, v 2 ) . In the past, the values of n and v were 10.5 and 3.8 respectively. The

    Using a hypothesis test at the 5% significance level, the manager finds that there is sufficient evidence
     to conclude that the new value of n is greater than 10.5.

     Stating a necessary assumption, find the smallest possible value of x.                               [4]
