```text
5  A car of mass 1500 kg is travelling along a straight horizontal road.

     (a)   It is given that there is a constant resistance to motion. The engine of the car is working at 24 kW while
          the car is travelling at a constant speed of 32 m s–1. The power is now increased to 28 kW.

         Find the acceleration of the car at the instant it is travelling at a speed of 36 m s–1.                   [4]

(b)   It is given instead that the resistance to the motion of the car is ( 340 + 4v)N when the speed of the car
      is v m s–1.

   When the engine is working at 20 kW, the car is travelling at constant speed.

    Find this constant speed.                                                                             [3]
```
