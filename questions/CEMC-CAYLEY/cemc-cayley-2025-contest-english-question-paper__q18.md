```text
18.  Lavinia has one bottle with capacity 6 L and another bottle with capacity 5 L. Both
     bottles are empty. She has a piece of paper with the following steps printed on it:

        (i) Fill the 6 L bottle completely with water.

        (ii) Pour water from the 6 L bottle into the 5 L bottle until the 5 L bottle is full.

       (iii) Empty the 5 L bottle.

      (iv) Pour water from the 6 L bottle into the 5 L bottle until the 5 L bottle is full or
         the 6 L bottle is empty, whichever happens ﬁrst.  If the 5 L bottle ﬁlls, empty
         the 5 L bottle and return to the beginning of this step.

    Lavinia completes this sequence of four steps in this order a total of 3 times. Lavinia
    never spills any water. How much water will be in the 5 L bottle after Lavinia is
    ﬁnished?

    (A) 0 L      (B) 1 L      (C) 2 L      (D) 3 L       (E) 4 L
```
