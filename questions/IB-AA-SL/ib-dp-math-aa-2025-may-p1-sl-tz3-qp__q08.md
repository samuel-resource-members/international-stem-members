     Consider the sequence  {un} , with  nth  term given by  un . The first three terms are
                                   u1 = k − 5 ,  u2 = 3 − 2k  and  u3 = 5k + 3 , where  k ∈  .
      (a)   Consider the case when  {un}  is arithmetic.

                 (i)   Find the value of  k .

                   (ii)   Hence, or otherwise, find  u3 .                                                                   [5]

      (b)   Consider the case where  k = 12 .

                 (i)   Show that the first three terms of  {un}  form a geometric sequence.
                   (ii)   Given that  {un}  is geometric, state a reason why the sum of an infinite number of
               terms of this sequence does not exist.                                                        [4]

       (c)  The sequence,  {un} , is geometric for a second value of  k .

                 (i)   Show that  k2 − 10k − 24 = 0 .

                   (ii)   Find the first three terms of  {un}  for this second value of  k .
                   (iii)  Hence, write down the value of  S2m , the sum of the first  2m  terms, for this second
                value of  k .                                                                                        [7]
