Problem 1.  For a positive integer N, let c1 < c2 <   < cm be all the positive integers smaller                                                                                        · · ·
than N that are coprime to N. Find all N ≥3 such that
                                   gcd(N, ci + ci+1) ̸= 1
for all 1     ≤i ≤m −1.
Here gcd(a, b) is the largest positive integer that divides both a and b. Integers a and b are coprime if
gcd(a, b) = 1.
