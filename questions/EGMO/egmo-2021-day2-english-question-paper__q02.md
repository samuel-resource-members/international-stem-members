## Text (OCR)

```text
Problem 5. A plane has a special point O called the origin. Let P be a set of 2021 points in the
plane such that
   (i) no three points in P lie on a line and
  (ii) no two points in P lie on a line through the origin.
A triangle with vertices in P is fat if O is strictly inside the triangle. Find the maximum number of
fat triangles.
```
