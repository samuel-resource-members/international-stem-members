Problem 2.  Let D and E be points in the interiors of sides AB and AC, respectively, of a triangle
ABC, such that DB = BC = CE. Let the lines CD and BE meet at F. Prove that the incentre
I of triangle ABC, the orthocentre H of triangle DEF and the midpoint M of the arc BAC of the
circumcircle of triangle ABC are collinear.
