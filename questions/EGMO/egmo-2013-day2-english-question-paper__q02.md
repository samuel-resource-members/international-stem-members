Problem 5.  Let Ωbe the circumcircle of the triangle ABC. The circle ω is tangent to the sides
AC and BC, and it is internally tangent to the circle Ωat the point P. A line parallel to AB and
intersecting the interior of triangle ABC is tangent to ω at Q.
