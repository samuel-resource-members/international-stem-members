Problem 3.  Let ABCDEF be a convex hexagon such that ∠A = ∠C = ∠E and ∠B = ∠D = ∠F
and the (interior) angle bisectors of ∠A, ∠C, and ∠E are concurrent.
Prove that the (interior) angle bisectors of ∠B, ∠D, and ∠F must also be concurrent.

Note that ∠A = ∠FAB. The other interior angles of the hexagon are similarly described.
