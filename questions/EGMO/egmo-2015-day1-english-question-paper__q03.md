Problem 3.  Let n, m be integers greater than 1, and let a1, a2, . . . , am be positive integers not
greater than nm. Prove that there exist positive integers b1, b2, . . . , bm not greater than n, such that

                             gcd(a1 + b1, a2 + b2, . . . , am + bm) < n,

where gcd(x1, x2, . . . , xm) denotes the greatest common divisor of x1, x2, . . . , xm.





Language: English                                             Time: 4 hours and 30 minutes
                                                        Each problem is worth 7 points
