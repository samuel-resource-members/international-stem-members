Problem 5.  Consider the triangle ABC with ∠BCA > 90◦. The circumcircle Γ of ABC has radius
R. There is a point P in the interior of the line segment AB such that PB = PC and the length of
PA is R. The perpendicular bisector of PB intersects Γ at the points D and E.
Prove that P is the incentre of triangle CDE.
