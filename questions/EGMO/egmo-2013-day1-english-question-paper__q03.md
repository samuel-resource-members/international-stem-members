Problem 3.  Let n be a positive integer.
(a) Prove that there exists a set S of 6n pairwise diﬀerent positive integers, such that the least common
    multiple of any two elements of S is no larger than 32n2.
(b) Prove that every set T of 6n pairwise diﬀerent positive integers contains two elements the least
   common multiple of which is larger than 9n2.
