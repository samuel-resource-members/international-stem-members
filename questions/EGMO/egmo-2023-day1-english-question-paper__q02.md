## Text (OCR)

```text
Problem 2. We are given an acute triangle ABC. Let D be the point on its circumcircle such that
AD is a diameter. Suppose that points K and L lie on segments AB and AC, respectively, and that
DK and DL are tangent to circle AKL.
Show that line KL passes through the orthocentre of ABC.
The orthocentre of a triangle is the point of intersection of its altitudes.
```
