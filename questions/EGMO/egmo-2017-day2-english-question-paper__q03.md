Problem 6.  Let ABC be an acute-angled triangle in which no two sides have the same length. The
reﬂections of the centroid G and the circumcentre O of ABC in its sides BC, CA, AB are denoted by
G1, G2, G3, and O1, O2, O3, respectively. Show that the circumcircles of the triangles G1G2C, G1G3B,
G2G3A, O1O2C, O1O3B, O2O3A and ABC have a common point.

The centroid of a triangle is the intersection point of the three medians. A median is a line connecting
a vertex of the triangle to the midpoint of the opposite side.
