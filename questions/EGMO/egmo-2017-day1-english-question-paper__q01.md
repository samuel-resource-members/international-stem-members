## Text (OCR)

```text
Problem 1.  Let ABCD be a convex quadrilateral with ∠DAB = ∠BCD = 90◦and ∠ABC >
∠CDA. Let Q and R be points on segments BC and CD, respectively, such that line QR intersects
lines AB and AD at points P and S, respectively.  It is given that PQ = RS. Let the midpoint of
BD be M and the midpoint of QR be N. Prove that the points M, N, A and C lie on a circle.
```
