4.   Which row correctly matches the level of structure of a protein molecule with the bonds that
      stabilize it?

           Level of structure  Bonds
     A.     primary              peptide and hydrogen bonds between amine and
                                 carboxyl groups

     D.     quaternary           covalent bonds between hydrophobic and hydrophilic
