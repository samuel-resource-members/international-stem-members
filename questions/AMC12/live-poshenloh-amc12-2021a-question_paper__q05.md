5. When a student multiplied the number 66 by the repeating decimal

                                    1. ab = 1.ababab … ,

   where a and b are digits, he did not notice the notation and just multiplied 66 by the
    terminating decimal 1.ab. Later he found that his answer was 0.5 less than the correct

     A   15

    B   30

    C   45

    D   60
