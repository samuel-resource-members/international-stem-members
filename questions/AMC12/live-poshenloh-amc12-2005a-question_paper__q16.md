## Statement

Three circles of radius \(s\) are drawn in the first quadrant of the \(xy\)-plane. The first circle is tangent to both axes, the second is tangent to the first circle and the \(x\)-axis, and the third is tangent to the first circle and the \(y\)-axis. A circle of radius \(r \gt s\) is tangent to both axes and to the second and third circles. What is \(\frac{r}{s}?\)
