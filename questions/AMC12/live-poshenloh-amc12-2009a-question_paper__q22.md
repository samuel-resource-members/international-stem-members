## Statement

A regular octahedron has side length \(1.\) A plane parallel to two of its opposite faces cuts the octahedron into two congruent solids. The polygon formed by the intersection of the plane and the octahedron has area \(\dfrac{a\sqrt{b}}{c},\) where \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c\) are positive integers, \(a\) and \(c\) are relatively prime, and \(b\) is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is \(a + b + c?\)
