7.  In a certain alien world, the maximum running speed v of an organism is dependent on
     its number of toes n and number of eyes m. The relationship can be expressed as v =
   kn a m b centimeters per hour, where k,a, and b are integer constants. In a population
   where all organisms have 5 toes, log v = 4 + 2 log m; and in a population where all
   organisms have 25 eyes, log v = 4 + 4 log n, where the logarithms are base 10. What
