## Statement

Driving at a constant speed, Sharon usually takes \(180\) minutes to drive from her house to her mother’s house. One day Sharon begins the drive at her usual speed, but after driving \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) of the way, she hits a bad snowstorm and reduces her speed by \(20\) miles per hour. This time the trip takes her a total of \(276\) minutes. How many miles is the drive from Sharon’s house to her mother’s house?
