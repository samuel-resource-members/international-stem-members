## Statement

A sequence of complex numbers \(z_0,\) \(z_1,\) \(z_2,\) \(\ldots\) is defined by the rule \[ z_{n+1} = \dfrac{i z_n}{\overline{z_n}}, \] where \(\overline{z_n}\) is the complex conjugate of \(z_n\) and \(i^2 = -1.\) Suppose that \(|z_0| = 1\) and \(z_{2005} = 1.\) How many possible values are there for \(z_0?\)
