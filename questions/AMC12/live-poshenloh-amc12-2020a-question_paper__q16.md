## Statement

A point is chosen at random within the square in the coordinate plane whose vertices are \((0, 0),\) \((2020, 0),\) \((2020, 2020),\) and \((0, 2020).\) The probability that the point is within \(d\) units of a lattice point is \(\tfrac12.\) (A point \((x, y)\) is a lattice point if \(x\) and \(y\) are both integers.) What is \(d\) to the nearest tenth?
