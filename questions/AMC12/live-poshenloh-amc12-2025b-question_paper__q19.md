19. A rectangular grid of squares has 141 rows and 91 columns. Each square has room for
   two numbers. Horace and Vera each fill in the grid by putting the numbers from 1
   through 141 × 91 = 12,831 into the squares. Horace fills the grid horizontally: he puts
   1 through 91 in order from left to right into row 1, puts 92 through 182 into row 2 in

   through 282 into column 2 in order from top to bottom, and continues similarly through
   column 91. How many squares get two copies of the same number?
