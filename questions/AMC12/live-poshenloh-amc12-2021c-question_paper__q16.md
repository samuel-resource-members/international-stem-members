```text
16. An organization has 30 employees, 20 of whom have a brand A computer while the
   other 10 have a brand B computer. For security, the computers can only be connected
   to each other and only by cables. The cables can only connect a brand A computer to a
   brand B computer. Employees can communicate with each other if their computers

   are directly connected by a cable or by relaying messages through a series of
   connected computers. Initially, no computer is connected to any other. A technician
    arbitrarily selects one computer of each brand and installs a cable between them,
   provided there is not already a cable between that pair. The technician stops once
   every employee can communicate with each other. What is the maximum possible
   number of cables used?

     A   190

    B   191

    C   192

    D   195

     E   196
```
