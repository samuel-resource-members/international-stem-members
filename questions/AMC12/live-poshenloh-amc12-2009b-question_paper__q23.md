## Statement

A region \(S\) in the complex plane is defined by \[ \scriptsize S = \{x + iy : -1 \le x \le 1,\ -1 \le y \le 1\}. \]

A complex number \(z = x + iy\) is chosen uniformly at random from \(S.\) What is the probability that \(\left(\dfrac{3}{4} + \dfrac{3}{4}i\right)z\) is also in \(S?\)
