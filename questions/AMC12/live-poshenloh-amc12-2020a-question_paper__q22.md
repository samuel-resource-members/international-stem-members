## Statement

Let \((a_n)\) and \((b_n)\) be the sequences of real numbers such that \[(2 + i)^n = a_n + b_n i\] for all integers \(n \ge 0,\) where \(i = \sqrt{-1}.\) What is \[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{a_n b_n}{7^n}?\]
