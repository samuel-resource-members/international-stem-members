## Statement

Let \(S\) be the set of ordered triples \((x, y, z)\) of real numbers for which \[ \log_{10}(x + y) = z \] and \[ \log_{10}(x^2 + y^2) = z + 1. \] There are real numbers \(a\) and \(b\) such that for all ordered triples \((x, y, z)\) in \(S\) we have \(x^3 + y^3 = a \cdot 10^{3z} + b \cdot 10^{2z}.\) What is the value of \(a + b?\)
