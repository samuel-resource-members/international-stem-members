## Statement

Quadrilateral \(ABCD\) has right angles at \(B\) and \(C,\) \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle BCD,\) and \(AB \gt BC.\) There is a point \(E\) in the interior of \(ABCD\) such that \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle CEB\) and the area of \(\triangle AED\) is \(17\) times the area of \(\triangle CEB.\) What is \(\dfrac{AB}{BC}?\)
