## Statement

Let \(f(z) = \dfrac{z + a}{z + b}\) and \(g(z) = f(f(z)),\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are complex numbers. Suppose that \(|a| = 1\) and \(g(g(z)) = z\) for all \(z\) for which \(g(g(z))\) is defined. What is the difference between the largest and smallest possible values of \(|b|?\)
