## Statement

A lattice point in an \(xy\)-coordinate system is any point \((x, y)\) where both \(x\) and \(y\) are integers. The graph of \(y=mx+2\) passes through no lattice point with \(0 \lt x \le 100\) for all \(m\) such that \(\dfrac{1}{2} \lt m \lt a.\) What is the maximum possible value of \(a?\)
