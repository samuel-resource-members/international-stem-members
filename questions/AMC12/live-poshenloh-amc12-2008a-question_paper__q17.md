## Statement

Let \(a_1,\) \(a_2,\) \(\ldots\) be a sequence of integers determined by the rule \(a_n = \frac{a_{n-1}}{2}\) if \(a_{n-1}\) is even and \(a_n = 3a_{n-1} + 1\) if \(a_{n-1}\) is odd. For how many positive integers \(a_1 \le 2008\) is it true that \(a_1\) is less than each of \(a_2,\) \(a_3,\) and \(a_4?\)
