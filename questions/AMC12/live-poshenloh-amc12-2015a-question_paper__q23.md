## Statement

Let \(S\) be a square of side length \(1.\) Two points are chosen independently at random on the sides of \(S.\) The probability that the straight-line distance between the points is at least \(\dfrac12\) is \(\dfrac{a - b\pi}{c},\) where \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c\) are positive integers and \(\gcd(a, b, c) = 1.\) What is \(a + b + c?\)
