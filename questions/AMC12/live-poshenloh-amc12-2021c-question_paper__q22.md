22. Azar and Carl play a game of tic-tac-toe. Azar places an X in one of the boxes in a 3 -
   by- 3 array of boxes, then Carl places an O in one of the remaining boxes. After that,
    Azar places an X in one of the remaining boxes, and so on until all 9 boxes are filled or
   one of the players has 3 of their symbols in a row — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal —
   whichever comes first, in which case that player wins the game. Suppose the players
   make their moves at random, rather than trying to follow a rational strategy, and that
    Carl wins the game when he places his third O. How many ways can the board look
    after the game is over?

     A   36

     B   112

    C   120

    D   148

     E   160
