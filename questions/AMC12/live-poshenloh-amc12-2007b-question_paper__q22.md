## Statement

Two particles move along the edges of equilateral \(\triangle ABC\) in the direction \[A\to B\to C\to A,\] starting simultaneously and moving at the same speed. One starts at \(A,\) and the other starts at the midpoint of \(\overline{BC}.\) The midpoint of the line segment joining the two particles traces out a path that encloses a region \(R.\) What is the ratio of the area of \(R\) to the area of \(\triangle ABC?\)
