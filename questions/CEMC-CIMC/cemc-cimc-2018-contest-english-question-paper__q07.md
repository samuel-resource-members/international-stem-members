## Text (OCR)

```text
The range of a list of numbers is the diﬀerence between the largest number and the
smallest number in the list. For example, the range of the list 1, 5, 1, 6, 3 is 6−1 = 5.
(a) Determine the range of the list 7, 13, 4, 9, 6.
(b) The list 11, 5, a, 13, 10 has a range of 12. Determine the two possible values
of a.
(c) The list 6+2x2, 6+4x2, 6, 6+5x2 has a range of 80. Determine the two possible
values of x.
(d) The list 5x + 3y, 0, x + y, 3x + y has a range of 19. If x and y are integers with
x > 0 and y > 0, determine the values of x and y.
```
