## Text (OCR)

```text
For a four-digit positive integer ABCD, where A, B, C, and D are digits with A ̸= 0
and D ̸= 0, we deﬁne the Reverse Digit sum (RD sum) of ABCD to be the sum of
ABCD and DCBA. For example, the RD sum of 1205 is 1205 + 5021 = 6226, while
the integer 2300 does not have an RD sum.
Note that the four-digit positive integer ABCD is equal to 1000A+100B +10C +D.
(a) Determine the RD sum of 4281.
(b) There are positive integers m and n with the property that the RD sum of the
integer ABCD is always equal to m × (A + D) + n × (B + C). State the value
of m and the value of n.
(c) Determine the number of four-digit integers whose RD sum is 3883.
(d) Determine the number of four-digit integers that are equal to the RD sum of a
four-digit integer.
```
