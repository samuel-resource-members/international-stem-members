3. ABCD is a quadrilateral inscribed in a circle of radius r. The diagonals AC,
BD meet at E.
Prove that if AC is perpendicular to BD then
EA? + EB? + EC? + ED* = 4r?, (*)
Is it true that if (*) holds then AC is perpendicular to BD? Give a reason for
your answer.
(6 marks]
