AB, AC, AD are three edges of a cube. AC is produced to E so
that AE = 2AC and AD is produced to F so that AF = 3AD.
Prove that the area of the section of the cube by any plane parallel
to BCD is equal to the area of the section of tetrahedron ABEF
by the same plane.
