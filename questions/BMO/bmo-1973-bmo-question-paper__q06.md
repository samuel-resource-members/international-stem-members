## Text (OCR)

```text
In answering general knowledge questions (framed so that each
question is answered yes or no) the teacher's probability of
being correct is a and a pupil's probability of being correct
is 8 or y according as the pupil is a boy or a girl.
The probability of a randomly chosen pupil agreeing with the
teacher's answer is 3.
Find the ratio of the numbers of boys to girls in the class.
```
