2. Points P, Q lie on the sides AB, AC respectively of triangle ABC and
are distinct from A. The lengths AP, AQ are denoted byx,y
respectively, with the convention that x > Oif P is on the same side
of A as B, and x <0 on the opposite side; similarly for y. Show
that PQ passes through the centroid of the triangle if and only if
Bxy = bx +cy
where b=AC, c=AB.
