## Text (OCR)

```text
3. Use the pigeonhole principle (i.e. if more than n objects are put into
n pigeonholes then at least one pigeonhole must contain more than one
object) to answer the following question.
A disc S is defined as the set of all points P in a plane such that
|OP| <¢ 1, where |oP| is the distance of P from 0, a given point in the
plane, called the centre of the disc.
Prove that if the disc S contains 7 points such that the distance from
any of the 7 points to any other is greater than or equal to 1, then one of
the 7 points is 0.
```
