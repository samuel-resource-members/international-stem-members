A sphere with centre 0 and radius r is cut in acircle K by a
horizontal plane distant 4r above 0, The part of the sphere above the
plane is removed and replaced by a right circular cone having K as its
base and having its vertex V ata distance 2r vertically above 0.
Q is a point on the sphere on the same horizontal level as 0. The plane
OVQ cuts the circle K in two points X and Y, of which Y is the
further from Q. P isa point of the cone lying on VY, whose position
can be determined by the fact that the shortest path from P to Q over
the surfaces of cone and sphere cuts the circle K at an angle of 45°,
Prove VP = v3r/V¥(1+1//5).

and the sides are measured by the angles they subtend at 0. You may find
