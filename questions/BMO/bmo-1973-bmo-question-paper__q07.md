## Text (OCR)

```text
The life-table issued by the Registrar-General of Draconia
shows out of 10,000 live births the number (y) expected
to be alive x years later. When x = 60, y = 4,820.
When x = 80, y = 3,205. For 60 «< x < 100 the curve
y = Ax (100 - x) +3 fits the figures in the table
(x - 40)?
very closely, A and B being constants.
Determine the life-expectancy (in years correct to one
decimal place) of a Draconian aged 70.
N.B. At age 100 all Draconians are put to death.
```
