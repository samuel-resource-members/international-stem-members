8. The interior of a wine glass is a right circular cone. The glass is
half filled with water and then slowly tilted so that the water starts and
continues to spill from a point P on the rim. What fraction of the whole
conical interior is occupied by water when the horizontal plane of the
water level bisects the generator of the cone furthest from P?
