You have a large number of congruent triangular equilateral discs
on a table and you want to fit discs together to make a convex
equiangular hexagon (i.e. one whose interior angles are each 120°).
Obviously n cannot be ony positive integer. The smallest n is 6,
the next smallest is 10 and the next 13. Determine conditions for
possible n.
n= 13
