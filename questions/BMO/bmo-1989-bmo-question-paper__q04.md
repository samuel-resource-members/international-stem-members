## Text (OCR)

```text
Consider the following triangle of numbers:
NwrRe
Aner
Qanr
oH
an
Each number is the sum of three numbers in the previous row: the
number above it and the numbers immediately to the left and right of
that number. If there is no number in one or more of these positions,
0 is used.
Prove that, from the third row on, every row contains at least one
even number.
```
