3.    The prime factorization of 784 is 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 7 × 7 or 24 × 72, and so 784 is a perfect
      square because it can be written in the form (22 ×7)×(22 ×7). The prime factorization
       of 45 is 32 × 5, and so 45 is not a perfect square. However, 45 × 5 is a perfect square
       since 45 × 5 = 32 × 52 = (3 × 5) × (3 × 5).
          (a) What are all positive integers j with j ≤20 for which 23 × 32 × j is a perfect
            square?

         (b) Determine all positive integers k so that 20 × k is both a perfect square and a
              divisor of 3600.
          (c) Determine the number of ordered triples of positive integers (a, b, c) so that
            a2 × b2 × c = 2025.
