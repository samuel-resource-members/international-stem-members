```text
4.    Parallelogram ABCD has vertices A(0, 0), B(7, 0), C(9, 4), and D(2, 4).

          (a) Point E(4, 4) lies on CD. What is the sum of the areas of △ABC, △ABD and
        △ABE?

         (b) Let G be a point with integer coordinates that lies on the perimeter of ABCD.
           Suppose △CDG has non-zero area. How many possibilities are there for the
            point G?

          (c) Determine the sum of the areas of all triangles whose vertices all have integer
             coordinates and lie on the perimeter of ABCD.
```
