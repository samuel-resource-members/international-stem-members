## Text (OCR)

```text
1.    At Monday’s practice, Lonnie sprints 200 m a total of 24 times. At Tuesday’s practice,
      he sprints 240 m a total of 20 times. On both days, he rests for 30 s between each
      consecutive pair of sprints. Lonnie sprints at a constant speed of 8 m/s.

          (a) On Monday, how many times does Lonnie take the 30 s rest between consecutive
             pairs of sprints?

         (b) On Monday, determine Lonnie’s total practice time. That is, determine the total
          number of seconds between the start of his ﬁrst sprint and the end of his last
              sprint, including the rests.

          (c) Determine how many fewer seconds Tuesday’s practice will take compared to
           Monday’s practice.
```
