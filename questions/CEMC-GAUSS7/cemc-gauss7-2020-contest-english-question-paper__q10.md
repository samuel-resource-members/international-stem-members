10. The positive divisors of 12 (other than itself) are 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6.  Their sum,
    1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 6, is greater than 12. An abundant number is a number for which the
   sum of its positive divisors (other than itself) is greater than the number itself. This
