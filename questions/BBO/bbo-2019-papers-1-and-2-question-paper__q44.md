Q45

Male and female bees visit distinct species of flower.

      Males only need nectar for energy, but females need both nectar and pollen
        for nesting.

     Which of the following is true?

   A. Male and female bees are different species.
   B. Male and female bees have identical adaptations.
   C. Male bees visit fewer types of flower than females.
   D. Male and female bees equally prefer the same colour patterns.
   E. This behaviour increases intraspecific competition.
