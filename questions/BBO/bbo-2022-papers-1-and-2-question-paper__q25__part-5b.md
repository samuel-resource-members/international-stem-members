Trypanosomes are single-celled parasites found across Africa. Almost all people are 
 naturally immune to *Trypanosoma brucei brucei* due to a protein in our blood called

 resistant to *T. gambiense*.To investigate why, scientists measured the likelihood of people 
 with different APOL1 alleles becoming sick with *T. gambiense* after they get infected.
