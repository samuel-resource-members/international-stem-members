Q20

The speed at which an action potential travels down a neuronal axon depends on the
       following:

      For myelinated neurons, the speed is directly proportional to the axon radius.

      For unmyelinated neurons, the speed is directly proportional to the square
       root of the axon radius.

Which of these axons conducts action potentials at the highest speed?

   A. A myelinated axon of radius: r
   B. An unmyelinated axon of radius: r/2
   C. An unmyelinated axon of radius: r²
   D. A myelinated axon of radius: r√2
