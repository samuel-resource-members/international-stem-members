Q28

Lichens are organisms formed by the symbiosis of several kingdoms of life. They
       often include filamentous fungi, yeasts, algae and cyanobacteria
       (photosynthetic bacteria) living together.



      Order the symbionts by their role in the organism.

       1) Fixes nitrogen
       2) Makes multicellular structure
       3) Primary producer
       4) Has chloroplasts
       5) Reproduces sexually


   A. Fungi
   B. Cyanobacteria
   C. Algae
   D. Cyanobacteria & algae
   E. Fungi & algae
