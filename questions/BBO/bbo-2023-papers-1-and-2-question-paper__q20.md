spread by eating infected meat. It can spread asexually within infected mammals, but tends

(e.g. mice, rats) to make them unafraid of cats; infected mice will run repeatedly toward
cats.In humans, and other non-prey mammals, *T. gondii* has less obvious and more
controversial effects, but is widely reported to increase recklessness, road-rage etc. Up to
1/3 of humans are infected with *T. gondii*, especially where rare meat is eaten.
