Root anatomy changes as plants mature. As roots grow, lateral roots grow from the primary
(roots). Many lateral roots can grow from one primary root, and each lateral root grows from
a single primordium. A primordium can be thought of as a lateral root bud. To study plant
root growth, scientists grew *Arabidopsis thaliana* plants for 10 days. Each day they
marked how far the root grew, and how many root primordia there were within each days'
growth. Every day they checked whether or not the primordia had developed into lateral
roots.
