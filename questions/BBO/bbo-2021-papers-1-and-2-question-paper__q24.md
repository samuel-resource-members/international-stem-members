*This question assesses your numerical skills with unfamiliar biology* Keiber’s law

animal's mass (*mass⁰ᐧ⁷⁵*).  Thus, a dog having a mass 100 times that of a mouse
will consume only about 32 times more energy than the mouse at rest.
