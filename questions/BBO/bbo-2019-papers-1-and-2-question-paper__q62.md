Q63

RuBisCO is the enzyme which photosynthesising organisms use to fix carbon
       dioxide (CO2) from the air to carbohydrates. However, RuBisCO can bind
      oxygen (O2) instead of CO2, which forces the organism to consume energy.

     Which of the following is true?

   A. Plants do not need O₂ to grow.
   B.  If RuBisCO were modified to prevent oxygen binding, crops would grow
       better.
   C. When RuBisCO first evolved, oxygen binding was as common as today.
   D. Increased atmospheric carbon dioxide levels are increasing the rate of oxygen
      binding to RuBisCO.
   E. Increasing the amount of RuBisCO in crops will help them grow better.
