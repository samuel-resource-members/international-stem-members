Q32

Fruit flies are the best organism for studying genetics. Wild type flies have a yellow
      body, whereas a mutant has an ebony body.

    A yellow male and an ebony female were crossed. Their progeny had a
      roughly equal number of yellow and ebony flies of both sexes.

     Which of the following is true?

   A. Inheritance of body colour is NOT mendelian.
   B. The mutation is X-linked.
   C. One parent was homozygous and one parent was heterozygous.
   D. Both parents were homozygous.
   E. Both parents were heterozygous.
