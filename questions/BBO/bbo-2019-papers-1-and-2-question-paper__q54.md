Q55

HIV virus has a single stranded RNA genome which becomes incorporated into the
     human genome and then produces new virus.





      Put the following processes of the HIV life-cycle in order. Assume the HIV
     genome has just entered the cell.


   A. DNA-dependent RNA polymerisation (transcription)
   B. RNA-dependent DNA polymerisation (reverse transcription)
   C. Release of new virus
   D. DNA-dependent DNA polymerisation (DNA replication)
