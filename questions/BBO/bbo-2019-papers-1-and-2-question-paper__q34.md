```text
Q35

Scottish wildcats are an endangered species.

       In a modern version of a mark recapture experiment, hundreds of motion
       sensitive cameras were used to take pictures of wildcats. Volunteers used the
       patterns of their fur to distinguish individual cats. In one year, they found 10
       different wildcats. In the next year they found 9 different wildcats, one of which
     had the same markings as a cat found the year before.

      Calculate how many wildcats are left in Scotland.

   A. 9
   B. 90
   C. 900
   D. 1111
   E. 9000
```
