Q57

The heart can be stimulated to contract with either more or less force by nerves and
      hormones.

     The heart can be stimulated to contract either more or less frequently by
      nerves.

     Which of the following is true?

   A.  If pressure receptors of the arteries are over-stimulated, heart force and
      speed will increase.
   B. Hearts rely more on the changing of frequency than force after they are
       transplanted into a new person.
   C. The right-side of the heart contracts with more force than the left-side of the
       heart.
   D. Blockage of the arteries requires the heart to contract more frequently.
   E. Blockage of the arteries requires the heart to contract with more force.
