Q51

A certain kind of blood cancer (leukaemia) is always caused when pieces of two
     chromosomes are swapped, which causes two genes to be stuck together.
      This new fusion gene produces a protein which forces cells to divide.

    DNA from different chromosomes can be stained different colours, and seen
     down a microscope. Chromosomes from a person with this blood cancer are
      shown.





     Which of the following is true?

   A. This mutation is recessive.
   B. Pieces of chromosome 8 and 3 are swapped.
   C. This person is female.
   D. This person has Down syndrome (an extra copy of chromosome 21).
   E. This person is heterozygous for the mutation.
