```text
Q58

Synapses transmit signals from one neuron to another, allowing them to process
       information like a computer. Different kinds of synapses have different effects
     on the neurons they signal to.

     The effect of stimulation from synapses 1, 2 and 3 on the electrical potential of
      neuron 4 was measured.





      Put the following events in order of the size of the potential that they cause at
       4. Put the event which causes the highest potential first, and the one that
      causes the most negative potential last.

   A. Stimulation from synapse 1, 2 and 3 simultaneously.
   B. Stimulation from synapse 2 twice in quick succession.
   C. Stimulation from synapse 1 and 3 simultaneously.
   D. Stimulation from synapse 1 three times in quick succession.
```
