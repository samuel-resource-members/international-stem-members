```text
Q47

You are familiar with food chains showing the flow of energy from primary producers
       to consumers, which mainly follows the flow of carbon.

      Construct a food chain based on the flow of nitrogen.

      Put the organism which consumes nitrogen gas from the air first, and the one
      which uses the nitrogen last at the end.

   A. Plants
   B. Anaerobically respiring gut bacteria
   C. Saprotrophic bacteria (saprobionts)
   D. Nitrogen fixing bacteria
   E. Animals
```
