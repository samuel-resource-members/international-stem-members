Q27

Plants use starch to store glucose, whilst animals store glycogen. Starch and
      glycogen stores have different properties which reflect the different ways
       plants and animals function.

     Which of the following is NOT a correct explanation of the difference between
       plant and animal stores?

   A. Animals need to release glucose quicker than plants to contract muscles
   B. Animals store most of their energy as fat
   C. Animals need to store their glucose in a denser form than plants
   D. Animals need to control the concentration of glucose in their fluids more than
       plants
   E. Animals often synthesise glucose from amino acids
