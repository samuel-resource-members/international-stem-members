The following logic puzzles are based on **defining characteristics** and **shared traits**
of branches of the tree of life. For example, vertebrate animals share many characteristics
(pairs of appendages, spinal cords, digestive tract below backbone), some of which may be
unique to them, but they are *defined* by having a backbone divided into jointed vertebrae.
