Q33

The colour of snapdragon flowers is genetically controlled as shown:

       Genotype          Phenotype
            rr                 White
        Rr                 Pink
      RR             Red


      Parent plants are crossed to produce many offspring. None of these first-
      generation offspring have white flowers. However, when these offspring are
      crossed, some of the second generation have white flowers.

     What colour could each of the parent flowers be? Choose from options in the
       table:

        Cross              Parent 1            Parent 2
                  i                  White           Red
                  ii                   Pink               Pink
                  iii                  Pink            Red
           iv                  Pink               White


   A.  i only
   B.  ii only
   C.  iii only
   D. iv only
   E.  i and ii
   F.   i and iii
   G.  i and iv
   H.  ii and iii
     I.    ii and iv
    J.   iii and iv
