Question 4

Part 1 of 3
Many organisms alter their physiology to suit the environment.Iron is an
essential nutrient but bio-available iron is scarce. *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*
is a bacteria which can survive in both low- and high-iron environments. It
can infect humans, but also live outside. The liver produces an iron chelating
protein called transferrin. Transferrin keeps the concentration of free-iron in
the blood very low.*P. aeruginosa* can create two siderophores.
Siderophores are iron chelators which are released into the environment
where they can bind iron, diffuse back towards the bacterium and be
reabsorbed.* Pyoverdine has a very low K *~d* (dissociation constant) and is
'expensive' to make.* Pyocheline has a high K *~d* and is 'cheap' to make.
