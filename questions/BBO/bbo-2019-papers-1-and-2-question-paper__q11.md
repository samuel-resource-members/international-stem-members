Q11

An enzyme was extracted from a bacterium native to Antarctica. The enzyme was
      mixed with an excess of substrates and the rate of reaction over time was
      measured.





     What could have happened at time A?

      A. The substrates ran out
      B. An excess of non-competitive (allosteric) inhibitor was added
      C. The reaction tube was placed in an ice-water bath
      D. A large volume of water was added to the reaction
