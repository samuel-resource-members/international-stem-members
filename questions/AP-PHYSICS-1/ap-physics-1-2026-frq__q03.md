3. A group of students are investigating friction using the following procedure. The students
  release a block of unknown mass near the top of a curved ramp. The block slides down the
  ramp. The block then transitions to a horizontal surface, as shown in Figure 1. Frictional forces
  are negligible between the block and the curved ramp, but there is friction between the block
  and the horizontal surface.

                                       Figure 1





  The students are asked to perform an experiment in which a single quantity is varied in order
  to collect data that could be graphed to determine the value n k of the coefficient of kinetic
   friction between the block and the horizontal surface. The students have access to only a
  meterstick.

  Part A

            i. Indicate quantities that could be measured by the students that would allow them to
        determine n k using a linear graph.
          ii. Briefly describe a method to reduce experimental uncertainty for the measured
          quantities.

  Part B

            i. Indicate what quantities the students could graph on the horizontal and vertical axes to
         create a linear graph that can be used to determine n k . Clearly state which quantity will
        be graphed on each axis.
          ii. Briefly describe the relationship between n k and a feature of the graph from part B
              (i). Your answer may include an equation that relates n k and the chosen feature of the
         graph.
