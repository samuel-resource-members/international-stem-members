11 50 cm3 of 1.0 mol dm–3 H2SO4 is added to 100 cm3 of 1.0 mol dm–3 NaOH in an insulated vessel.

    Both solutions are at a temperature of 20 °C before mixing. After mixing, the temperature rises
   and the highest temperature reached is 29 °C.

   Assume that:

          ●    all the energy released in the reaction goes into raising the temperature of the
              aqueous reaction mixture

          ●   the specific heat capacity of the mixture is 4.2 J cm–3 K–1.

   What is the value of the enthalpy of neutralisation determined from this experiment?

   A  –113.4 kJ mol–1

   B  –56.7 kJ mol–1

   C  –37.8 kJ mol–1

   D  –18.9 kJ mol–1
