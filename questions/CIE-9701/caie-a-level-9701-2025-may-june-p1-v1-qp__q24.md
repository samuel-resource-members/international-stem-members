24 Compound X contains two Period 3 elements, Y and Z.

   Compound X reacts with water to form only two products: a slightly soluble hydroxide and
   compound Q.

  Q is a compound of element Z and hydrogen.

   Compound Q burns in moist  air to produce an oxide and water. The oxidation number of
