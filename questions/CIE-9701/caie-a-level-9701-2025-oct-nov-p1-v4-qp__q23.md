23 The oxides of nitrogen, NO and NO2, act as pollutants in the Earth’s atmosphere in a number of
     different ways.

    Three statements about the oxides of nitrogen are listed.

          1  They react with oxygen and water vapour to form  nitric acid, a constituent of
                 acid rain.

          2  They react with carbon monoxide to form photochemical smog.

          3  They catalyse the oxidation of sulfur dioxide in the formation of sulfuric acid, another
                  constituent of acid rain.

   Which statements are correct?

   A   1, 2 and 3    B  1 and 2 only  C  1 and 3 only  D  2 and 3 only
