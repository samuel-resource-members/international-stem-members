     (a) Method

             temperature in the space for results.

           •     Fill one burette with FB 5. Label the burette FB 5.
           •     Fill the other burette with distilled water.

        Experiment 1

           •   Use the 10 cm3 pipette to transfer 10.0 cm3 of FB 4 into the cup.
           •   Add 9.00 cm3 of distilled water from the burette to the same cup.
