     (a) Method

       ●  Use the measuring cylinder to transfer 30.0 cm3 of FA 2 into a conical flask.
       ●   Weigh the conical flask containing FA 2. Record the mass.
       ●   Weigh the container with FA 1. Record the mass.
       ●   Tip all the FA 1 slowly into the conical flask. When the reaction slows, swirl the flask
               gently.
       ●   Weigh the container with any residual FA 1. Record the mass.
       ●   Calculate the mass of FA 1 added. Record the mass.
       ●   Leave the conical flask and its contents for 15 minutes. Swirl the flask occasionally during
                this time.

                      During this period begin work on Question 2.
