Question B2

Broken Vase

A uniformly charged ring of radius d has total charge Q and is fixed in place. A point charge −q of
mass m is placed at its center. Both Q and q are positive. As a result, if the point charge is given a
small velocity along the ring’s axis of symmetry, it will oscillate about the ring’s center.

a. Find the period T of the oscillations.

For the rest of the problem, we will consider this situation in a reference frame where the ring is
moving along its axis of symmetry with a constant speed v, which may be comparable to the speed
of light c. In this frame, the ring and point charge have charge Q′ and q′, and the point charge still
oscillates about the ring’s center.

b. What is the period T ′ of oscillation of the point charge in this frame?

c. When the charge is a small distance ∆x from the center of the ring, find the restoring force in
   terms of q′, Q′, v, d, ∆x, and fundamental constants.

d. Suppose the restoring force has the form F = −k ∆x. Find the period of the resulting oscillations
   in terms of k, m, v, and fundamental constants.

e. Suppose the electric charge transforms between reference frames as Q′ = γnQ and q′ = γnq. By
   combining your answers to parts (c) and (d), and comparing to part (b), find the value of n.

To solve this problem, you will need the following results from relativity:

   • The Lorentz factor is defined as γ = 1/p1 −v2/c2.

   • The momentum of a particle is p = γmv, and m is the same in all frames.

   • The electromagnetic force on a charge q is F = dp/dt = q(E + v × B).

   • The electric field of a charge q at the origin with constant velocity v is radial, with magnitude

                                          q        1 −v2/c2
                     E =
                                      4πϵ0r2 (1 −(v2/c2) sin2 θ)3/2

     where θ is the angle of r to v.
