Ping Pong

A thin wire of negligible resistance and total length D is wound to form a thin cylindrical solenoid
of length ` ⌧D. A conducting sphere of radius R ⌧` is attached to each end of the solenoid.
Initially there is no current in the wire, and the spheres have charges Q and −Q. Give all youranswers in terms of R, `, D, and the speed of light c = 1/pµ0✏0.
