Two large parallel plates of area A are placed at x = 0 and x = d ≪  A in a semiconductor
medium. The plate at x = 0 is grounded, and the plate at x = d is at a ﬁxed potential −V0, where
V0 > 0. Particles of positive charge q ﬂow between the two plates. You may neglect any dielectric
eﬀects of the medium.

a. For large V0, the velocity of the positive charges is determined by a strong drag force, so that

                                          v = µE

      i. In the steady state, there is a nonzero but time-independent density of charges between the

      relationship between ρ(x), v(x), and their derivatives.
