Question B1

A uniform solid spherical ball starts from rest on a loop-the-loop track.  It rolls without slipping
along the track. However, it does not have enough speed to make it to the top of the loop. From
what height h would the ball need to start in order to land at point P directly underneath the top
of the loop? Express your answer in terms of R, the radius of the loop. Assume that the radius of
the ball is very small compared to the radius of the loop, and that there are no energy losses due
to friction.
