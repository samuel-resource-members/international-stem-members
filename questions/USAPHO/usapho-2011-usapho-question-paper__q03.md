```text
Question A3

A light bulb has a solid cylindrical ﬁlament of length L and radius a, and consumes power P.
You are to design a new light bulb, using a cylindrical ﬁlament of the same material, operating at
the same voltage, and emitting the same spectrum of light, which will consume power nP. What
are the length and radius of the new ﬁlament? Assume that the temperature of the ﬁlament is
approximately uniform across its cross-section; the ﬁlament doesn’t emit light from the ends; and
energy loss due to convection is minimal.
```
