Question A3

Two stars, each of mass M and separated by a distance d, orbit about their center of mass. A
planetoid of mass m (m ≪M) moves along the axis of this system perpendicular to the orbital
plane.

   Let Tp be the period of simple harmonic motion for the planetoid for small displacements from
the center of mass along the z-axis, and let Ts be the period of motion for the two stars. Determine
