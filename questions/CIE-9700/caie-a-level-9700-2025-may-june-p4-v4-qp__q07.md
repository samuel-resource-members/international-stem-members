7   The grass Oryza sativa is grown as a food crop to produce rice. Rice plants are adapted to grow
     with their roots submerged in water.

     (a) An experiment was carried out to investigate the development of aerenchyma tissue in the
         roots of rice plants.

           •   10-day old seedlings were grown with their roots submerged in water.
           •   Some were grown in water that was kept oxygenated and others were grown in water
               that did not contain oxygen (deoxygenated).
           •    At 12 hour intervals, some seedlings from each group were removed and transverse
             sections of their roots were prepared and examined.
           •   The sections were cut at the same distances from the root tip to check for development.
           •   The percentage of root tissue that had developed into aerenchyma tissue was calculated.
