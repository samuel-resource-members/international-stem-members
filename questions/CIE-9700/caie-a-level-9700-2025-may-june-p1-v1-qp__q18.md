18 A student immersed a plant tissue in solution S.

    For the next 30 minutes, the student used a microscope to observe the cells in the plant tissue.

    During this time, the cytoplasm of the cells shrunk and no longer pushed up against the cell wall.
     In many cells, a space appeared between the cytoplasm and the cell wall.

   Which statements are correct conclusions from these observations?

          1  The water potential in solution S was more negative than the water potential in the
               cytoplasm of the cells at the start of the experiment.

          2   Without a cell wall to provide support,  it is likely that many of the cells would have
                   burst.

          3  The clear space between the cell wall and the cytoplasm of many of the cells was
                       filled with air.
