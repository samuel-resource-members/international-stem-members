24.  There are functions f(x) with the following properties:
       • f(x) = ax2 + bx + c for some integers a, b and c with a > 0, and

       • f(p) = f(q) = 17 and f(p+q) = 47 for some prime numbers p and q with p < q.

    For each such function, the value of f(pq) is calculated. The sum of all possible values
     of f(pq) is S. What are the rightmost two digits of S?
