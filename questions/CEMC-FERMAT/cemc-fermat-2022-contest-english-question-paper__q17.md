17. An aluminum can in the shape of a cylinder is closed at both ends. Its surface area
      is 300 cm2. If the radius of the can were doubled, its surface area would be 900 cm2.
      If instead the height of the can were doubled, what would its surface area be?
    (The surface area of a cylinder with radius r and height h is equal to 2πr2 + 2πrh.)
    (A) 450 cm2   (B) 600 cm2   (C) 750 cm2   (D) 375 cm2   (E) 300 cm2
