```text
21. A sequence has terms a1, a2, a3, . . .. The ﬁrst term is a1 = x and the third term is
     a3 = y. The terms of the sequence have the property that every term after the ﬁrst
    term is equal to 1 less than the sum of the terms immediately before and after it.
    That is, when n ≥1, an+1 = an + an+2 −1. The sum of the ﬁrst 2018 terms in the
    sequence is

    (A) −x −2y + 2023          (B) 3x −2y + 2017          (C) y
    (D) x + y −1               (E) 2x + y + 2015
```
