16.  If n is a positive integer, the notation n! (read “n factorial”) is used to represent the
    product of the integers from 1 to n. That is, n! = n(n −1)(n −2) · · · (3)(2)(1). For
    example, 4! = 4(3)(2)(1) = 24 and 1! = 1. If a and b are positive integers with b > a,
    the ones (units) digit of b! −a! cannot be

    (A) 1        (B) 3        (C) 5        (D) 7        (E) 9
