```text
18.  Four teams play in a tournament in which each team plays exactly one game against
    each of the other three teams. At the end of each game, either the two teams tie
     or one team wins and the other team loses. A team is awarded 3 points for a win,
    0 points for a loss, and 1 point for a tie. If S is the sum of the points of the four teams
     after the tournament is complete, which of the following values can S not equal?

    (A) 13       (B) 17       (C) 11       (D) 16       (E) 15
```
