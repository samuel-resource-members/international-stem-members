3.  The sequence A, with terms a1, a2, a3, . . ., is deﬁned by
                                  an = 2n, for n ≥1.

   The sequence B, with terms b1, b2, b3, . . ., is deﬁned by
                          b1 = 1, b2 = 1, and bn = bn−1 + 2bn−2, for n ≥3.

    For example, b3 = b2 + 2b1 = 1 + 2(1) = 3.

    In this question, the following facts may be helpful:

       • A geometric sequence is a sequence in which each term after the ﬁrst is obtained
        from the previous term by multiplying it by a non-zero constant called the common
           ratio. For example, 3, 6, 12 is a geometric sequence with three terms and common
          ratio 2.

       • The sum of the ﬁrst n terms of a geometric sequence with ﬁrst term a, and common
                             1 −rn
          ratio r ̸= 1, equals a                .
                             1 −r
          (a) What are the 5th terms for each sequence? That is, what are the values of a5
           and b5?
         (b) For some real numbers p and q, bn = p · (an) + q · (−1)n for all n ≥1. (You do
            not need to show this.) What are the values of p and q?

          (c) Let  Sn  be  the sum  of  the  ﬁrst n  terms  in  sequence B.   That   is,
