4.  Let x0 be a non-negative integer. For each integer i ≥0, deﬁne xi+1 = (xi)2 + 1.

          (a) Show that x2 −x0 is even for all possible values of x0.

         (b) Show that x2026 −x2020 is divisible by 10 for all possible values of x0.

          (c) Parsa chooses an integer n with 1 ≤n ≤100 at random and sets x0 = n.
           Determine the probability that x115 −110 is divisible by 105.
