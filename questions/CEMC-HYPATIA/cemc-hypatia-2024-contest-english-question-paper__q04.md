```text
4.   A Hewitt number is a positive integer that is the sum of the cubes of three consecutive
       positive integers. The smallest Hewitt number is 13 + 23 + 33 = 36.

          (a) How many Hewitt numbers between 10 000 and 100 000 are divisible by 10?

         (b) Determine how many of the smallest 2024 Hewitt numbers are divisible by 216.

          (c) Consider the following statement:

                There are two distinct Hewitt numbers whose sum is equal to 9 · 2k
                    for some positive integer k.

          Show that this statement is true by ﬁnding two such Hewitt numbers or prove
            that it is false by demonstrating that there cannot be two such Hewitt numbers.
```
