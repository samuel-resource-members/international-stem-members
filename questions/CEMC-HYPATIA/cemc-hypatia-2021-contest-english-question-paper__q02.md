2.  Trapezoid ABCD has vertices A(0, 0), B(12, 0), C(11, 5), D(2, 5).

          (a) What is the area of trapezoid ABCD?

         (b) The line passing through B and D intersects the y-axis at the point E. What
             are the coordinates of E?
          (c) The sides AD and BC are extended to intersect at the point F. Determine the
             coordinates of F.
         (d) Determine all possible points P that lie on the line passing through B and D, so
            that the area of △PAB is 42.
