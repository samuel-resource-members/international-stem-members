2.    For a positive 3-digit integer n, f(n) is equal to the sum of n and the digits of n. For
      example, f(351) = 351 + 3 + 5 + 1 = 360.

          (a) What is the value of f(132)?

         (b) If f(n) = 175, what is the value of n?

          (c) If f(n) = 204, determine all possible values of n.
