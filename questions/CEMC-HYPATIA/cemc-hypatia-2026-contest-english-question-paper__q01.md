1.      If points are collinear, then they lie on the same straight line.

          (a) If the points A(1, 2), B(2, 5) and C(3, c) are collinear, what is the value of c?

         (b) Three distinct points D(0, 7), E and F(14, 0) are collinear. If the coordinates of
