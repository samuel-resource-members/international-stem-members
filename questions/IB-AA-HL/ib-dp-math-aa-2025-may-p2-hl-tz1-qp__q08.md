Consider the functions  f , g  and  h  defined as follows for  t ∈  .
f (t ) = sin (2t + 1)
g (t ) = sin (2t + 3)
h (t ) = f (t ) + g (t )
Show that  h (t ) = Im (e2ti(ei + e3i)) .
(a)
[2]
Write  ei + e3i  in the form  reiq , where  r > 0  and  -p < q ≤ p .
(b)
[2]
Hence or otherwise, write  h (t )  in the form  p sin (2t + q) , where  p > 0  and  0 < q < 2p .
(c)
