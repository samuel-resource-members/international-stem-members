In a study, measurements for arm span,  A cm , and foot length,  F cm , are taken from a large
group of adults.
For this group, the regression line of  F  on  A  is found to be  F = 0.335A - 32.6 , and
the regression line of  A  on  F  is found to be  A = 2.89F + 99.3 . Each regression line passes
through the mean point.
(a)
By using an appropriate regression line, find an estimate of the arm span for an adult
with a foot length of  19.8 cm .
[2]
(b)
For this group of adults, find the mean arm span and the mean foot length.
[3]
The heights,  H cm , of adults in the group can be modelled by a normal distribution with mean
163 cm  and standard deviation  s cm .
It is found that  88 %  of the group have a height between  153 cm  and  173 cm .
Find the value of  s .
(c)
[3]
