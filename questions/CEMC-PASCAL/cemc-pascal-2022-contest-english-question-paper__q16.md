16.  Alvin, Bingyi and Cheska play a two-player game that never ends in a tie. In a recent
    tournament between the three players, a total of 60 games were played and each pair
     of players played the same number of games.

        • When Alvin and Bingyi played, Alvin won 20% of the games.

        • When Bingyi and Cheska played, Bingyi won 60% of the games.

        • When Cheska and Alvin played, Cheska won 40% of the games.

   How many games did Bingyi win?
