```text
54. Two substances with the formula (en)2CoCl3 (en =
     ethylenediamine, H2NCH2CH2NH2) have different
    aqueous solubilities and different UV-visible spectra in
    aqueous solution. Which is the most likely explanation
     for this observation?

    (A) The substances are enantiomers.

    (B) The substances are geometric isomers.

    (C) One substance contains [(en)2CoCl2]Cl with six-
         coordinate cobalt and the other contains (en)2CoCl3
         molecules with seven-coordinate cobalt.

    (D) One substance contains [(en)2CoCl2]Cl with six-
         coordinate cobalt and the other contains
         [(en)2Co]Cl3 with four-coordinate cobalt.
```
