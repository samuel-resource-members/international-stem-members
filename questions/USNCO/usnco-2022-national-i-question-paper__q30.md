```text
30. The reaction between A and B involves an intermediate I
    whose concentration remains very low throughout the
     reaction. The initial rates of a reaction as a function of
    [B] (at constant [A]) are shown below. Which statement
    about the mechanism is most consistent with the data?





    (A) A initially reacts reversibly to form the intermediate
             I, which then reacts irreversibly with B in a process
           that is first-order in B.
    (B) A initially reacts reversibly to form the intermediate
             I, which then reacts irreversibly with B in a process
           that is second-order in B.
    (C) B initially reacts reversibly in a first-order process to
        form the intermediate I, which then reacts
          irreversibly with A.
    (D) B initially reacts reversibly in a second-order
         process to form the intermediate I, which then reacts
          irreversibly with A.
```
