16. The melting point of an organic compound X is 5.0 °C.
   How do the vapor pressures of X(s) and X(l) compare at
    10.0 °C?

    (A) The vapor pressure of X(s) is less than the vapor
          pressure of X(l).

    (B) The vapor pressure of X(s) is equal to the vapor
          pressure of X(l).

    (C) The vapor pressure of X(s) is greater than the vapor
          pressure of X(l).

    (D) The relative vapor pressures cannot be determined
         without knowledge of the relative densities of X(s)
        and X(l).
