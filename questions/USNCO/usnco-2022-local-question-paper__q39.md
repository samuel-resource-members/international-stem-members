```text
39. A galvanic cell is constructed under standard conditions
     using cobalt in cobalt(II) nitrate solution and indium in
     indium(III) nitrate solution. Which statements about this
     cell are correct?
                  Half-reaction                   E°, V
            Co2+(aq) + 2e– → Co(s)            –0.28
              In3+(aq) + 3e– → In(s)             –0.34
           I.   The standard cell potential is 0.46 V.
         II.    Indium is the anode.
    (A)  I only                 (B)  II only
    (C) Both I and II           (D)  Neither I nor II
```
