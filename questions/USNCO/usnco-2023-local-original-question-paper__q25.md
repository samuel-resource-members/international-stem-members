25. Hydrogen and nitrogen react according to the following
     equation:
                  N2(g) + 3 H2(g) → 2 NH3(g)

      If the concentration of H2(g) is changing at a rate of –12
    mol L-1 s-1, at what rate is the concentration of NH3(g)
    changing?

    (C)  +8.0 mol L-1 s-1        (D) +12 mol L-1 s-1
