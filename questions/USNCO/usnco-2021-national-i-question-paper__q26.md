26.  In a reaction A + 2 B → C + D, initial rates were
    measured as shown in the table. What is the initial rate
     of the reaction with [A]0 = 0.200 M and [B]0 = 0.400 M?

              [A]0, M    [B]0, M     Initial rate, M s-1
             0.120     0.300       1.80 × 10-5
             0.180     0.450       4.05 × 10-5
             0.080     0.200       8.00 × 10-6
             0.200     0.400           ???
    (A)  3.20 × 10-5 M s-1
    (B)  4.00 × 10-5 M s-1
    (C)  5.00 × 10-5 M s-1
    (D)  It cannot be determined from the information given.
