```text
31. The reaction of nitrogen and hydrogen to form ammonia
      is maintained at equilibrium in a rigid container at fixed
     temperature:
                 N2(g) + 3 H2(g)     2 NH3(g)
    Helium gas is introduced into the system, causing the
     total pressure to increase by a factor of two. How will
     the equilibrium for the reaction shift?

    (A)  It will be unaffected.

    (B)  It will shift to the left.

    (C)  It will shift to the right.

    (D) The direction that the equilibrium will shift depends
        on whether the reaction is endothermic or
         exothermic.
```
