1.  Adipic acid, C6H10O4 (M = 146.15), is prepared by the
    partial oxidation of cyclohexane, C6H12 (M = 84.16),
   according to the following balanced reaction:
       2 C6H12(l) + 5 O2(g) → 2 C6H10O4(s) + 2 H2O(l)
  A sample of 25.0 g cyclohexane reacts with excess
   oxygen to give 33.5 g of adipic acid. What is the yield of
    the reaction?

   (A) 38.6%   (B) 39.9%   (C) 48.9%   (D) 77.2%
