31.  Pure PCl5 is placed in a rigid flask so that its pressure at
    250 °C is 2.000 bar. The gas slowly decomposes to PCl3
    and Cl2 at this temperature, and the partial pressure of Cl2
      is eventually 0.814 bar. What is the value of Kp for the
     reaction?

           PCl5(g) ⇄ PCl3(g) + Cl2(g)       Kp = ???

    (A)  0.559    (B)  0.686    (C)  0.814    (D)  1.78
