27. The iodination of acetone is irreversible and has a rate
    law of Rate = k[CH3COCH3][H+].
               CH3COCH3(aq) + I3 –(aq) ®
            CH3COCH2I(aq) + H+(aq) + 2 I–(aq)

   When the reaction is carried out with an initial
     concentration of I3 – much smaller than those of either
     acetone or H+, it is observed that the yellow color due to
      I3 – fades over time, finally disappearing abruptly at a time
       t. How does t depend on the initial concentrations?
     Dependence on: [CH3COCH3]     [H+]          [I3 –]

    (A)                Direct          Direct     None

    (B)                Inverse        Inverse    None

    (C)                Direct          Direct      Inverse

    (D)                Inverse        Inverse     Direct
