```text
24. A certain volume of NaOH(aq) is titrated against
    HCl(aq) in a well-insulated vessel with constant stirring.
    Each solution is initially at 22.0 °C, and the temperature
     of the reaction mixture is monitored after each 2.0 mL of
     hydrochloric acid is added.





    The data in closed circles correspond to an experiment
    conducted with 20.0 mL NaOH solution and 1.0 M HCl.
    The open circles were carried out using the same
     concentration but a different volume of NaOH solution,
    and using a different concentration of aqueous HCl.
    Which volume of NaOH and concentration of HCl
    correspond to the conditions that give the open circles?

                    Volume NaOH   Concentration HCl
     Closed circles:       20 mL              1.0 M
     Open circles:
           (A)           40 mL              2.0 M
            (B)           30 mL              2.0 M
           (C)           15 mL              0.5 M
           (D)           10 mL              0.5 M
```
