```text
35. A mixture containing CO(g), H2O(g), CO2(g), and H2(g)
      is allowed to reach equilibrium at 120 °C in a container
     that maintains a constant total pressure of 1.0 bar.

             CO(g) + H2O(g) ⇌ CO2(g) + H2(g)

     5.0 g of H2O is added to this container, which is allowed
     to reattain equilibrium (still at 120 °C and 1 bar total
     pressure). How does the partial pressure of H2(g) change
     as a result of the addition of H2O?
    (A) The partial pressure of H2(g) increases.

    (B) The partial pressure of H2(g) decreases.

    (C) The partial pressure of H2(g) does not change.

    (D) The effect on the partial pressure of H2(g) cannot be
         determined from the information given.
```
