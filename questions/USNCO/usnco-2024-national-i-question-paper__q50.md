50. Which statements about carbon-oxygen bond lengths are
     correct?
            I.  The carbon-oxygen bond in carbon dioxide, CO2,
               is shorter than the carbon-oxygen bond in
           carbonyl sulfide, COS.
          II.  The carbon-oxygen bond in phosgene, COCl2, is
            shorter than the carbon-oxygen bond in urea,
          CO(NH2)2.

    (C) Both I and II           (D)  Neither I nor II
