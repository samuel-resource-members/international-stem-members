24. The ∆H°f of CH4(g) is –74.6 kJ mol-1 and the bond
     dissociation enthalpies (BDEs) of several bonds are given
     in the table.

               C–C             347
              C–H            413
              H–H            432

    Based on these data, what is the molar enthalpy of
     sublimation of C(s,graphite) to form C(g)?
    (A) 713 kJ mol-1            (B) 788 kJ mol-1
    (C) 1061 kJ mol-1          (D) 1135 kJ mol-1
