2.  Potassium tris(oxalato)ferrate(III) trihydrate is prepared
   by precipitation from aqueous solution according to the
   following equation:

          3 K2C2O4(aq) + FeCl3(aq) + 3 H2O(l) ®
             K3Fe(C2O4)3 • 3H2O(s) + 3 KCl(aq)

  A student mixes 10.00 g K2C2O4 (M = 166.2) and 5.00 g
   FeCl3 (M = 162.2) in 20.0 mL water and isolates 5.74 g
   K3Fe(C2O4)3 • 3H2O by filtration. What is the percent
    yield of this reaction?

   (A) 19.4%   (B) 37.9%   (C) 58.3%   (D) 65.5%
