38. A standard galvanic cell is assembled from silver and
    platinum in 1 M solutions of the corresponding metal
     ions. Which metal would be labeled the cathode, and
    what is the voltage of the standard galvanic cell?

                   Half-reaction                  E°, V

                Pt2+(aq) + 2e– ® Pt(s)             1.20

             Ag+(aq) + e– ® Ag(s)             0.80

    (A) Ag cathode, E° = 1.60 V

    (B)  Pt cathode, E° = 1.20 V

    (C) Ag cathode, E° = 0.80 V

    (D)  Pt cathode, E° = 0.40 V
