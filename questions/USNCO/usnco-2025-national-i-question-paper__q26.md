26. The oxidation of cyanide ion to cyanate ion by
     hypoiodite is proposed to take place by the following
    mechanism.  If the pH is greater than either the pKa of
   HOI or of HCN, what is the rate law?
             OI– + H+ ⇌ HOI                           fast
       CN– + HOI ⇌ ICN + OH–        fast, unfavorable
       ICN + OH– → HOCN + I–                slow
    HOCN + OH– → OCN– + H2O                   fast

    (A)  Rate = k[CN–][OI–]

    (B)  Rate = k[CN–][OI–][H+]

    (C)  Rate = k[CN–][OI–][OH–]

    (D)  Rate = k[CN–][OI–][OH–]2
