23. The gas-phase bromination of propene has a standard
     enthalpy of reaction of –122.5 kJ mol-1.

                C3H6(g) + Br2(g) → C3H6Br2(g)
                ∆H° = –122.5 kJ mol-1

                 Substance    ∆H°f, kJ mol-1
                  C3H6(g)         20.4
                     Br2(g)         30.9

    What is the standard enthalpy of formation of
    C3H6Br2(g)?
    (A)  –71.2 kJ mol-1          (B)  –102.1 kJ mol-1
    (C)  –142.9 kJ mol-1        (D)  –173.8 kJ mol-1
