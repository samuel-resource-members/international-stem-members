56. Which is the best explanation for the higher reactivity of
    conjugated dienes relative to non-conjugated alkenes in
     electrophilic addition reactions?

          reaction with electrophiles while non-conjugated
         alkenes cannot.
    (B)  Conjugated dienes have more potentially reactive
           sites than do non-conjugated alkenes.
    (C) The π bonding in conjugated dienes is weaker than
          the π bonding in non-conjugated alkenes.
    (D)  Conjugated dienes are nonplanar while non-
         conjugated alkenes are planar.
