```text
13. A room temperature sample of xenon gas in a metal
     container with a piston has a volume of 1.00 L and a
     pressure of 4.0 atm. The piston is moved to change the
    volume of the container to 2.00 L, after which the
     pressure reads 1.75 atm. Which is the best explanation
     for this observation?

    (A) The volume is miscalibrated and the actual final
        volume is 1.75 L.

    (B) Some xenon has leaked out through the seal in the
          piston.

    (C) The friction of moving the piston has caused the
         temperature of the gas to increase.

    (D) The system is correctly calibrated, well sealed, and
         remains at room temperature.
```
