```text
29. An irreversible reaction A + B → C is carried out under
     various conditions, but always with [B]0 >> [A]0. Plots
     of 1/[A] as a function of time for each reaction are linear,
    and the slopes of these plots do not depend on the initial
     concentration of B. What is the rate law for the reaction
    under these conditions?
    (A)  Rate = k[A]            (B)  Rate = k[A][B]
    (C)  Rate = k[A]2           (D)  Rate = k[A]2[B]
```
