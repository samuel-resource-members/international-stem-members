```text
5. The hypochlorite concentration in bleach can be
   determined by treating it with excess iodide ion in acidic
    solution, which causes the formation of triiodide ion
   according to the following balanced reaction:
             OCl–(aq) + 3 I–(aq) + 2 H3O+(aq) →
                  Cl–(aq) + I3–(aq) + 3 H2O(l)
   The triiodide can then be titrated with sodium thiosulfate:
          I3–(aq) + 2 S2O32-(aq) → 3 I–(aq) + S4O62-(aq)
  A 75.0-mL sample of liquid bleach is treated with excess
   KI and acid, and then titrated with 0.0235 M sodium
    thiosulfate solution. The endpoint is observed on the
    addition of 21.23 mL of this solution. What is the
   hypochlorite concentration in the bleach?
   (A)  4.99 × 10-4 M          (B)  1.66 × 10-3 M
   (C)  3.33 × 10-3 M          (D)  6.65 × 10-3 M
```
