```text
29.  Nitrogen dioxide reacts with carbon monoxide with the
     rate law shown

             NO2(g) + CO(g) ® NO(g) + CO2(g)
                       Rate = k[NO2]2

    Which mechanisms are consistent with this rate law?

         I.     2 NO2 ⇄ N2O4                  fast, unfavorable
          N2O4 + CO ® N2O3 + CO2   slow
          N2O3 ® NO + NO2            fast
        II.    2 NO2 ® NO + NO3         slow
         NO3 + CO ® NO2 + CO2     fast

    (A)  I only                  (B)  II only

    (C) Both I and II           (D)  Neither I nor II
```
