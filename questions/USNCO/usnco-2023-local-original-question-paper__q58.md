58. Under certain conditions, HBr adds to 2-methylpropene
     to give predominantly 2-bromo-2-methylpropane rather
     than isomeric 1-bromo-2-methylpropane. Which is the
     best mechanistic explanation for this regioselectivity?

          carbocation.

          stable carbanion.

    (C) H• adds preferentially to C-1 to form the more stable
           radical.
