8. A solution is prepared by dissolving 10.00 g of a salt in
   90.00 g of water. What additional information is needed
    to calculate the molarity of this solution?
             I.  The molar mass of the salt
           II.  The density of the solution

   (A)  I alone would allow one to calculate the molarity.

   (B)  II alone would allow one to calculate the molarity.

   (C)  I and II together would allow one to calculate the
         molarity.

   (D) The molarity cannot be calculated even if both I and
           II are known.
