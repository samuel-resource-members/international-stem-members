18. Which statement about a sample of ammonia (NH3) at its
      triple point is correct?

    (A)  It has a pressure of 1.0 atm.

    (B)  It can be at only one possible temperature.

    (C)  There are equal amounts of NH3 in the solid, liquid,
        and gas phases.

         remain at the triple point when the volume of the
          container is decreased by a factor of two.
