36. Some solid silver nitrate is dissolved in 100.0 mL of 1.00
  M NH3 solution. To this solution, 50.0 mL of 1.00 M
    NaCl solution must be added to see the first sign of
     cloudiness. How much AgNO3 was present? The Ksp of
    AgCl is 1.8 × 10-10 and the Kf of Ag(NH3)2+ is 1.0 × 108.
    (A)  8.1 × 10-11 mol         (B)  2.1 × 10-3 mol
    (C)  3.2 × 10-3 mol          (D)  4.9 × 10-3 mol
