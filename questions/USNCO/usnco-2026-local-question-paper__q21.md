```text
21.  In a coal-fired power plant, coal is burned to generate
     heat that boils water. 2.5 kg of coal is burned to heat
    500.0 kg of water at 95 °C, ultimately converting 30.0 kg
     to steam. Which type of coal was burned? (For liquid
    water near 100 °C , Cp = 4.21 kJ kg–1 K–1 and ΔH°vap =
    2260 kJ kg–1.)

    (A)  Lignite, ∆H°combustion = 15 – 20 kJ g–1
    (B)  Sub-bituminous, ∆H°combustion = 23 – 28 kJ g–1
    (C)  Bituminous, ∆H°combustion = 26 – 30 kJ g–1
    (D)  Anthracite, ∆H°combustion = 30 – 33 kJ g–1
```
