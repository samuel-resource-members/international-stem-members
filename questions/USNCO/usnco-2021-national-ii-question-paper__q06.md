6.  [13%] Explain the following observations about complex ions of the transition metals.
          a.  The Cr(H2O)63+ ion is paramagnetic while the Sc(H2O)63+ ion is diamagnetic.
          b.  The CoF63– ion is paramagnetic while the Co(CN)63– ion is diamagnetic.
          c.  The NiCl42– ion is paramagnetic while the PtCl42– ion is diamagnetic.
          d.  The CoCl42– ion is strongly colored while the ZnCl42– ion is colorless.
          e.  The MnO4– ion is strongly colored while the ReO4– ion is colorless.
