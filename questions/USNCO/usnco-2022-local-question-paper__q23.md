```text
23. Two reactions have similar ∆H°rxn values (∆H°rxn (1) ≈
     ∆H°rxn (2)), but reaction (1) has a much smaller standard
    entropy change than reaction (2) (∆S°rxn (1) << ∆S°rxn (2)).
    At 298 K, which statements about these two reactions
    must be correct?
           I.    Reaction (1) must have a larger equilibrium
             constant (Keq (1) > Keq (2)).
         II.    Reaction (1) must have a larger Arrhenius
              prefactor (A(1) > A(2)).
    (A)  I only                 (B)  II only
    (C) Both I and II           (D)  Neither I nor II
```
