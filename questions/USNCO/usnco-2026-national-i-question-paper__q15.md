15. The vapor pressure of butane at 0 °C is 1 atm. A 0.100
    mol sample of liquid butane at 0 °C is introduced into an
     evacuated, well-insulated 2.25 L container. Which
     statements correctly describe the system after it reaches
     equilibrium?

           II.   All of the butane is in the gas phase.

    (C) Both I and II           (D)  Neither I nor II
