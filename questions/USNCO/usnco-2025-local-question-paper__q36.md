36. A solution is 0.05 M in each of the anions fluoride (F–),
     carbonate (CO32-), and phosphate (PO43-). As calcium ion
      is added slowly to this solution, in what order will the
     solids precipitate? The Ksp values are: CaF2, 3.9  10-11;
    CaCO3, 3.8  10-9; Ca3(PO4)2, 1.0  10-25.

    (A)  First CaF2, then CaCO3, then Ca3(PO4)2

    (B)  First CaF2, then Ca3(PO4)2, then CaCO3

    (C)  First Ca3(PO4)2, then CaF2, then CaCO3

    (D)  First Ca3(PO4)2, then CaCO3, then CaF2
