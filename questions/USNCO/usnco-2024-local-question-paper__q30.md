```text
30.  Chlorine gas catalyzes the gas-phase decomposition of
     nitrous oxide according to the equation
                   2 N2O ® 2 N2 + O2
    The following mechanism is proposed for the catalyzed
     reaction:
                        Cl2 ⇌ 2 Cl             fast, unfavorable
                Cl + N2O ® N2 + ClO               slow
             ClO + N2O ® N2 + ClO2                  fast
               ClO2 + Cl ® Cl2 + O2                    fast
    What rate law is predicted by this mechanism?
    (A)  Rate = k[N2O]          (B)  Rate = k[N2O]2
    (C)  Rate = k[N2O][Cl2]     (D)  Rate = k[N2O][Cl2]1/2
```
