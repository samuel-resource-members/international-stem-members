29. The irreversible reaction A + B ® C takes place by the
          following two-step mechanism involving an intermediate
      X whose concentration is low throughout the reaction:
                                       k1
                        A        X
                                       k–1
                                       k2
                      X + B       C

        Which statement cannot be correct regardless of the
          values of k1, k–1, and k2?

         (A) The reaction is 0th order in A.

         (B) The reaction is 1st order in A.

         (C) The reaction is 0th order in B.

         (D) The reaction is 1st order in B.
