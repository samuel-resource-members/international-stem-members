```text
22.  In a vacuum calorimetry experiment, high-energy plasma
     discharges between Ti(s) electrodes are used to
     electrolyze water in dilute KOH(aq). During an
     experiment, the electrical input is 500. kJ and the
     calorimeter records 610. kJ of heat release into the
     surroundings. Which best explains this experimental
     result?

    (A) The electrodes undergo partial oxidation, an
          additional reaction not accounted for in electrolysis.

    (B) The system’s large specific heat capacity increases
          the total heat registered per unit of electrical energy.

    (C) The overall process is endothermic, so the
         surroundings must release heat to maintain
          equilibrium.

    (D) The entropy increase from gas formation causes the
         system to do work, resulting in additional
         temperature rise.
```
