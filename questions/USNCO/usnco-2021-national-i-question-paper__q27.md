  27. The temperature-dependence of the rate constants of a
        series of six related reactions are studied, and the
       reactions are all found to have the same rate constant at
      370 K (their isokinetic temperature). Which statement
       best describes the relationship between the reactions’
       Arrhenius pre-factors A and their activation energies Ea?
      (A) The reactions share a common pre-factor A but
              differ in their activation energies Ea.
       (B) The reactions share a common activation energy Ea
            but differ in their pre-factors A.
      (C) As the reactions’ pre-factors A increase, their
             activation energies Ea increase.
      (D) As the reactions’ pre-factors A increase, their
             activation energies Ea decrease.
