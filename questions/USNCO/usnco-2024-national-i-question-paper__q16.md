16. Two compounds, A and B, are both solids at 20 °C. In
          separate experiments, one mole of each is heated and its
          temperature measured as a function of the amount of heat
          added. Which statement about the compounds is correct?





         (A) The molar heat capacity of solid A is greater than
                 that of solid B.

         (B) The molar heat capacity of liquid A is greater than
                 that of liquid B.

         (C) The melting point of A is less than that of B.

         (D) The heat of fusion of A is less than that of B.
