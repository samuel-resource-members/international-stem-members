```text
17. A pure sample of benzene (C6H6) melts sharply at 5.5 °C,
    while a pure sample of hexafluorobenzene (C6F6) melts
     sharply at 5.2 °C. However, an equimolar mixture of
    C6H6 and C6F6 melts sharply at 23.7 °C. Which is the
     best explanation for this observation?
    (A) C6H6 and C6F6 are immiscible in the liquid phase.
    (B) C6H6 and C6F6 form a crystal containing equal
        amounts of the two substances.
    (C)  Fluorocarbons characteristically show negative
          freezing point depression constants.
    (D) The enthalpy of mixing of C6H6 and C6F6 is
          negative.
```
