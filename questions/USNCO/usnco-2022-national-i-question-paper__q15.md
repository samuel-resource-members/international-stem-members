15. The triple point of ethyne, C2H2, is 192.4 K and 1.2 atm.
    What may be concluded from this information?
    (A)  Solid ethyne sublimes rather than melting at 1 atm
          pressure.
    (B)  Solid ethyne is less dense than liquid ethyne.
    (C)  Solid ethyne is not thermodynamically stable at
         193.0 K.
    (D) A sample of ethyne at 192.4 K must have a pressure
          of 1.2 atm.
