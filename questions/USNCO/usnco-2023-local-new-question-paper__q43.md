```text
43. Which statement best describes the amount of energy
     released by gas-phase oxygen and sulfur atoms upon
     accepting an electron (electron affinities)?
    (A) O releases less energy than S because the smaller
          size of O causes greater electron-electron repulsion.
    (B) O releases less energy than S because O has a lower
        mass than S.
    (C) O releases more energy than S because the electron
          enters a 2p orbital in O but a 3p orbital in S.
    (D) O releases more energy than S because O has a
          larger effective nuclear charge Zeff than S.
```
