```text
12. A sample of malachite, Cu2CO3(OH)2, is heated over a
    Bunsen burner to form CuO. The mass percent copper in
     the sample is calculated based on the mass before and
     after heating. Which could explain the experimentally
    determined percent copper being lower than that
    expected for malachite?

    (A) The sample is not heated as strongly as
        recommended in the procedure (not long or hot
         enough).

    (B) The sample is heated more strongly than
        recommended in the procedure (too long or too hot).

    (C)  Azurite (Cu3(CO3)2(OH)2) is present in the sample.

    (D)  Goethite (FeO(OH)) is present in the sample.
```
