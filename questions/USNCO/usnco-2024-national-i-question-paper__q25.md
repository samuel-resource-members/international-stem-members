```text
25.  For a reaction X + Y ® Z, data for three experiments are
     given.
         [X]0, M        [Y]0, M        Initial rate, M-1 min-1
          0.10           0.10             2.0 ´ 10-4
          0.30           0.10             6.0 ´ 10-4
          0.30           0.30             5.4 ´ 10-3

    Which mechanism is consistent with the above data?
     (Reactions shown as reversible are assumed to be rapid
    and unfavorable.)

    (A)    X + Y ® Z        (B)       2 X ⇌ X2
                                 X2 + Y ® Z + X
    (C)       2 Y ⇌ Y2        (D)    X + Y ⇌ XY
          Y2 + X ® Z + Y          XY ® Z
```
