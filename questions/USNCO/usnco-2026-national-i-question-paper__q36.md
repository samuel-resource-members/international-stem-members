```text
36. A solution initially is 0.10 M in both Cd2+ and Tl+ and is
     kept saturated with hydrogen sulfide gas ([H2S] = 0.1 M).
     In what pH range will one of the metal ions be
     precipitated quantitatively (> 99.9%) while the other
    remains completely in solution?
        Ksp of CdS = 1.0 ´ 10–27   Ksp of Tl2S = 6.0 ´ 10–22
       Ka of H2S = 8.9 ´ 10–8    Ka of HS– = 1.0 ´ 10–19

    (A) Between 0.5 and 6.8

    (B) Between 2.0 and 3.9

    (C) Between 4.0 and 6.8

    (D)  There is no pH at which this is possible.
```
