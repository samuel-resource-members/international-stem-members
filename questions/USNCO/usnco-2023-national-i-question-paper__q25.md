25.  Hydroxide ion reacts with methyl iodide to give methanol
     in an irreversible second-order reaction with a rate
     constant k = 6.5 ´ 10-5 L mol-1 s-1.

   How long will it take the concentration of CH3I to
     decrease from 0.010 M to 0.0020 M in a 1.00 M solution
     of sodium hydroxide?
