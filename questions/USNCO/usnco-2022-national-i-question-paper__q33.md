33. The Ksp of BaCrO4 is 1.2 × 10-10. What is the minimum
    volume of 1.5 × 10-5 M Na2CrO4 that would need to be
    added to 1.0 L of 1.5 × 10-5 M Ba(NO3)2 solution to
    induce precipitation of BaCrO4?

    (B)  0.73 L
    (C)  1.0 L

          precipitation of BaCrO4.
