25. The rate law for the oxidation of iodide to iodine by
    hydrogen peroxide in weakly acidic solution is studied by
     allowing the reaction to proceed in the presence of a
     fixed amount of sodium thiosulfate (which rapidly
     reduces iodine to iodide) and measuring the time t to the
      first appearance of a blue color due to the starch-iodine
    complex. The following data are obtained on solutions
    made with the indicated volumes of reactant solutions
    and enough water to give a total volume of 10.0 mL.
    What are the reaction orders in H2O2, I–, and H+?
       H2O2(aq) + 2 I–(aq) + 2 H+(aq) → I2(aq) + 2 H2O(l)

     Run   CH3COOH,   NaOH,    KI,    H2O2,         t, s
           mL      mL    mL   mL
      1        2.0         1.0      2.0     2.0     68.2
      2        4.0         1.0      2.0     2.0     68.9
      3        2.0         1.0      4.0     2.0     33.2
      4        2.0         1.0      2.0     4.0     32.9
          Order in H2O2    Order in I–   Order in H+

    (A)        0             1           1

    (B)        1             1           0

    (C)        1             1           1

    (D)        1             2           2
