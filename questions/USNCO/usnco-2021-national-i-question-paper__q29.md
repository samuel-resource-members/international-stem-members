```text
29. The hydrolysis of formamide to formate ion in basic
    aqueous solution is proposed to proceed by the following
     steps:

    HCONH2 + OH–  HCO(OH)(NH2)–    unfavorable
         HCO(OH)(NH2)– + OH–                      fast,
            HCO2(NH2)2– + H2O             unfavorable
        HCO2(NH2)2– → HCO2– + NH2–        irreversible
                                                                         fast,         NH2– + H2O → NH3 + OH–                                                     favorable

    Which of the following statements is necessarily true,
    given this mechanism?
    (A) The reaction is first-order in HCONH2.
    (B) The reaction is first-order in hydroxide ion.
    (C) HCO2– is an intermediate in this reaction.
    (D)  If the reaction is carried out in deuterium oxide, the
         formate ion will contain deuterium.
```
