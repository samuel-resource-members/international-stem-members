## Statement

How many four-digit positive integers \(abcd,\) with \(a \neq 0,\) have the property that the three two-digit integers \(ab < bc < cd\) form an increasing arithmetic sequence?

One such number is \(4692,\) where \(a=4,\) \(b=6,\) \(c=9,\) and \(d=2.\)
