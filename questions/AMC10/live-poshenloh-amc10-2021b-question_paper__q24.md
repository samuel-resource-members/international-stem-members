24. Arjun and Beth play a game in which they take turns removing one brick or two
    adjacent bricks from one “wall” among a set of several walls of bricks, with gaps
    possibly creating new walls. The walls are one brick tall. For example, a set of walls of
    sizes 4 and 2 can be changed into any of the following by one move: (3, 2),(2, 1, 2),
    (4),(4, 1),(2, 2), or (1, 1, 2).





    Arjun plays first, and the player who removes the last brick wins. For which starting
    configuration is there a strategy that guarantees a win for Beth?

     A    (6, 1, 1)

     B    (6, 2, 1)

    C    (6, 2, 2)

    D    (6, 3, 1)

     E    (6, 3, 2)
