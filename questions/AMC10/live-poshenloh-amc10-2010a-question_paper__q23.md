## Statement

Each of \(2010\) boxes in a line contains a single red marble, and for \(1 \le k \le 2010,\) the box in the \(k\)th position also contains \(k\) white marbles. Isabella begins at the first box and successively draws a single marble at random from each box, in order. She stops when she first draws a red marble. Let \(P(n)\) be the probability that Isabella stops after drawing exactly \(n\) marbles. What is the smallest value of \(n\) for which \(P(n) \lt \dfrac{1}{2010}?\)
