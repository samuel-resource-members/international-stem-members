## Statement

In rectangle \(PQRS,\) \(PQ = 8\) and \(QR = 6.\) Points \(A\) and \(B\) lie on \(PQ,\) points \(C\) and \(D\) lie on \(QR,\) points \(E\) and \(F\) lie on \(RS,\) and points \(G\) and \(H\) lie on \(SP\) so that \(AP = BQ < 4\) and the convex octagon \(ABCDEFGH\) is equilateral. The length of a side of this octagon can be expressed in the form \(k + m\sqrt{n},\) where \(k,\) \(m,\) and \(n\) are integers and \(n\) is not divisible by the square of any prime. What is \(k + m + n?\)
