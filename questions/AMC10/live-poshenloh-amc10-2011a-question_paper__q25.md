## Statement

Let \(R\) be a square region and \(n \geq 4\) an integer. A point \(X\) in the interior of \(R\) is called

\(n\)-ray partitional

if there are \(n\) rays emanating from \(X\) that divide \(R\) into \(n\) triangles of equal area. How many points are \(100\)-ray partitional but not \(60\)-ray partitional?
