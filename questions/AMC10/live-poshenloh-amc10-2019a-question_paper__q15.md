## Statement

A sequence of numbers is defined recursively by \(a_1 = 1,\) \(a_2 = \frac{3}{7},\) and \[a_n=\dfrac{a_{n-2} \cdot a_{n-1}}{2a_{n-2} - a_{n-1}}\] for all \(n \geq 3.\) Then \(a_{2019}\) can be written as \(\frac{p}{q},\) where \(p\) and \(q\) are relatively prime positive integers. What is \(p+q ?\)
