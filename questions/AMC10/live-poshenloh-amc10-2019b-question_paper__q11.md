## Statement

In Jar \(1\) the ratio of blue to green marbles is \(9:1,\) and the ratio of blue to green marbles in Jar \(2\) is \(8:1.\) There are \(95\) green marbles in all. How many more blue marbles are in Jar \(1\) than in Jar \(2?\)
