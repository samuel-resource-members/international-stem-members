## Statement

The remainder can be defined for all real numbers \(x\) and \(y\) with \(y \neq 0\) by \[\operatorname{rem}(x,y)=x-y\left\lfloor\frac{x}{y}\right\rfloor,\] where \(\left \lfloor \frac{x}{y} \right \rfloor\) denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to \(\frac{x}{y}.\) What is the value of \(\text{rem} \left(\frac{3}{8}, -\frac{2}{5} \right)?\)
