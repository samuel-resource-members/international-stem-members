## Statement

Samia set off on her bicycle to visit her friend, traveling at an average speed of \(17\) kilometers per hour. When she had gone half the distance to her friend’s house, a tire went flat, and she walked the rest of the way at \(5\) kilometers per hour.

In all, it took her \(44\) minutes to reach her friend’s house. In kilometers rounded to the nearest tenth, how far did Samia walk?
