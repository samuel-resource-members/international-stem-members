12. Which of the following conditions is sufficient to guarantee that integers x,y, and z
    satisfy the equation

                      x(x −y) + y(y −z) + z(z −x)

                      = 1?


     A    x > y and y = z

    B   x = y −1 and y = z −1

    C   x = z + 1 and y = x + 1

    D   x = z and y −1 = x

     E   x + y + z = 1
