\[ \begin{aligned} a \cdot b + 63 &= 20 \cdot \operatorname{lcm}(a, b) \\ &\quad {}+ 12 \cdot \gcd(a, b), \end{aligned} \]

where \(\gcd(a, b)\) denotes the greatest common divisor of \(a\) and \(b,\) and \(\operatorname{lcm}(a, b)\) denotes their least common multiple?
