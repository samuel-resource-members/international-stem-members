11. Emily sees a ship traveling at a constant speed along a straight section of a river. She
   walks parallel to the riverbank at a uniform rate faster than the ship. She counts 210
   equal steps walking from the back of the ship to the front. Walking in the opposite
    direction, she counts 42 steps of the same size from the front of the ship to the back.
    In terms of Emily’s equal steps, what is the length of the ship?

     A   70

    B   84

    C   98

    D   105

     E   126
