## Statement

Let \(a \gt 0,\) and let \(P(x)\) be a polynomial with integer coefficients such that \[ \begin{aligned} P(1) &= P(3) \\ &= P(5) = P(7) = a, \end{aligned} \] and \[ \begin{aligned} P(2)&=P(4)=P(6)\\ &=P(8)=-a. \end{aligned} \] What is the smallest possible value of \(a?\)
