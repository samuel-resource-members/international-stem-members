10. A school has 100 students and 5 teachers. In the first period, each student is taking
   one class, and each teacher is teaching one class. The enrollments in the classes are
   50,20,20,5, and 5. Let t be the average value obtained if a teacher is picked at
   random and the number of students in their class is noted. Let s be the average value

   obtained if a student was picked at random and the number of students in their class,
    including the student, is noted. What is t −s?

     A   −18.5

    B   −13.5

    C   0

    D    13.5

     E    18.5
