## Statement

Let \(a_1,\) \(a_2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(a_{2018}\) be a strictly increasing sequence of positive integers such that

\[a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_{2018} = 2018^{2018}.\]

What is the remainder when \(a_1^3 + a_2^3 + \cdots + a_{2018}^3\) is divided by \(6?\)
