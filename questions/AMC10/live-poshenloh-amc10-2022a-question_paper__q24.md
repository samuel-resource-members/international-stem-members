24. How many strings of length 5 formed from the digits 0,1,2,3,4, are there such that
    for each j ∈{1, 2, 3, 4}, at least j of the digits are less than j?

    (For example, 02214 satisfies this condition because it contains at least 1 digit less
    than 1, at least 2 digits less than 2, at least 3 digits less than 3, and at least 4 digits less
    than 4. The string 23404 does not satisfy the condition because it does not contain at
    least 2 digits less than 2. )

     A   500

     B   625

    C   1089

    D   1199

     E   1296
