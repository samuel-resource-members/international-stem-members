## Statement

Let \(p,\) \(q,\) and \(r\) be the distinct roots of the polynomial \[x^3 - 22x^2 + 80x - 67.\] There exist real numbers \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) such that \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{1}{s^3-22s^2+80s-67}\\ =\frac{A}{s-p}+\frac{B}{s-q}\\ \quad+\frac{C}{s-r} \end{gathered} \] for all real numbers \(s\) with \(s\notin\{p,q,r\}.\) What is \[\dfrac1A+\dfrac1B+\dfrac1C?\]
