16. Jerry likes to play with numbers. One day, he wrote all the integers from 1 to 2024 on
   the whiteboard. Then he repeatedly chose four numbers on the whiteboard, erased
   them, and replaced them by either their sum or their product. (For example, Jerry’s first
   step might have been to erase 1,2,3, and 5, and then write either 11, their sum, or 30,

    B   1011

    C   1012

    D   1013
