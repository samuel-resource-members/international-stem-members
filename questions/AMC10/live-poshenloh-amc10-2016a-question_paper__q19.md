## Statement

In rectangle \(ABCD,\) \(AB=6\) and \(BC=3.\) Point \(E\) between \(B\) and \(C,\) and point \(F\) between \(E\) and \(C\) are such that \(BE=EF=FC.\) Segments \(\overline{AE}\) and \(\overline{AF}\) intersect \(\overline{BD}\) at \(P\) and \(Q,\) respectively.

The ratio \(BP:PQ:QD\) can be written as \(r:s:t\) where the greatest common factor of \(r,\) \(s,\) and \(t\) is \(1.\) What is \(r+s+t?\)
