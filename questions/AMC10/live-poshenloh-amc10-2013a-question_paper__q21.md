## Statement

A group of \(12\) pirates agree to divide a treasure chest of gold coins among themselves as follows. The \(k^{\text{th}}\) pirate to take a share takes \(\dfrac{k}{12}\) of the coins that remain in the chest. The number of coins initially in the chest is the smallest number for which this arrangement will allow each pirate to receive a positive whole number of coins. How many coins does the \(12^{\text{th}}\) pirate receive?
