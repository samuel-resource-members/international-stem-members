```text
34  Giraffes are mammals that have long necks. This enables them to eat leaves from tall trees. One
    theory to explain how giraffes developed long necks is natural selection.

   Which statement is part of the explanation for the development of long necks in giraffes by
    natural selection?

   A   Giraffes with longer necks were better adapted to the environment than giraffes with shorter
        necks.

   B  Humans selected giraffes with the longest necks and bred them together.

   C  There was no competition for food.

   D  There were no trees in the environment.
```
