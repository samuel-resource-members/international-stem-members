30.  The methanol fuel cell relies on the oxidation of methanol by oxygen. The two half-equations are
     as follows:

                      Anode: CH3OH(l) + H2O(l) → CO2(g) + 6H+(aq) + 6e-

                           Cathode: 4H+(aq) + O2(g) + 4e- → 2H2O(l)

    What is the balanced equation for the overall reaction?

     A.   2CH3OH(l) + 3O2(g) → 2CO2(g) + 4H2O(l)

     B.   CH3OH(l) + 2O2(g) → CO2(g) + 2H2O(l)

     C.   2CO2(g) + 4H2O(l) → 2CH3OH(l) + 3O2(g)

     D.   CO2(g) + 2H2O(l) → CH3OH(l) + 2O2(g)
