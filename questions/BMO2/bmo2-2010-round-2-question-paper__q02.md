2. In triangle ABC the centroid is G and D is the midpoint of CA. The
    line through G parallel to BC meets AB at E. Prove that AEC =
 DGC if, and only if, ACB = 90◦. The centroid of a triangle is the
   intersection of the three medians, the lines which join each vertex to
   the midpoint of the opposite side.
