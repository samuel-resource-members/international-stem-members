## Text (OCR)

3. Two circles S1 and S2 touch each other externally at K; they
   also touch a circle S internally at A1 and A2 respectively. Let
  P be one point of intersection of S with the common tangent
   to S1 and S2 at K. The line PA1 meets S1 again at B1, and
  PA2 meets S2 again at B2. Prove that B1B2 is a common
   tangent to S1 and S2.
