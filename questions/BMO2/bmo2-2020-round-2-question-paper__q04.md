## Text (OCR)

```text
4. A sequence b1, b2, b3,. . . of nonzero real numbers has the property that

                                             b2  −1                                                 n+1
                                      bn+2 =
                                               bn

   for all positive integers n.

  Suppose that b1 = 1 and b2 = k where 1 < k < 2. Show that there is some constant B,
   depending on k, such that −B ≤bn ≤B for all n. Also show that, for some 1 < k < 2, there
    is a value of n such that bn > 2020.
```
