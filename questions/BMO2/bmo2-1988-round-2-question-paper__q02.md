## Text (OCR)

```text
2. Prove that the number of ways of arranging 2n distinguishable objects
in n pairs is
1.3.65. ... . (2n-1)
if the order of the pairs and the order of the objects within each pair
are immaterial. Eg for 4 objects a, b, c, d the 3 pairings are
ab, cd; ac, bd; ad, be.
A party of 10 people consisting of 5 married couples is to be split into
5 pairs. A pair may consist of two men, two women or a man and a woman ,
but must not be a married couple. In how many ways can this arrangement
be made if order, as above, is immaterial? Explain your reasoning
carefully.
```
