AT.
In a colony of (m+1) mice, prove that at least one of the following
statements is true:
(a) There is a set A of (m+1) mice none of which is a parent of
any other in the set.
(b) ‘here is an ordered set B of (n+ 1) mice 8g 98g 9-6 AA]
such that aig is a parent of ay for each i=1,2,...,n.
