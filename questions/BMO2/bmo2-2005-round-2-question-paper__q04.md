4. Let X = {A1, A2, . . . , An} be a set of distinct 3-element subsets of
   {1, 2, . . . , 36} such that
    i)  Ai and Aj have non-empty intersection for every i, j.

   Show that n ≤100. How many such sets X are there when n = 100?
