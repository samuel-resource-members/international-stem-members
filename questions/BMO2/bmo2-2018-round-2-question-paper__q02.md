## Text (OCR)

```text
2. There are n places set for tea around a circular table, and every place
   has a small cake on a plate. Alice arrives ﬁrst, sits at the table, and
   eats her cake (but it isn’t very nice). Next the Mad Hatter arrives,
   and tells Alice that she will have a lonely tea party, and that she must
   keep on changing her seat, and each time she must eat the cake in
   front of her (if it has not yet been eaten).  In fact the Mad Hatter
    is very bossy, and tells Alice that, for i = 1, 2, . . . , n −1, when she
   moves for the i-th time, she must move ai places and he hands Alice
   the list of instructions a1, a2, . . . , an−1. Alice does not like the cakes,
   and she is free to choose, at every stage, whether to move clockwise or
   anticlockwise. For which values of n can the Mad Hatter force Alice
   to eat all the cakes?
```
