## Text (OCR)

```text
A 'figure-of-eight' curve, S, consists of two touching circles of equal
radii. Show that a pair of two distinct congruent hexagons (not necessarily
convex) exists with the following properties:
(a) All the vertices of the hexagons lie on S.
(b) Neither hexagon has all its vertices on one circle.
(c) Neither hexagon can be obtained from the other by a single
translation, a single rotation or a single reflection.
```
