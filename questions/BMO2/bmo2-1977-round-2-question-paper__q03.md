3. EITHER (a) Prove that if n is any given integer then the equation
Oxy + l7yz + 27zx =n
has a solution in integers x,y,z.
OR (b) Prove that in the arithmetic progression
a, atd, at+2d, ...., atnd, ....
where a,d are positive integers, there exists an infinite set of terms having the
same prime divisors.
