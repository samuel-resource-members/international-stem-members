4. In the land of Hexagonia, the six cities are connected by a rail network
   such that there is a direct rail line connecting each pair of cities. On
   Sundays, some lines may be closed for repair. The passengers’ rail
   charter stipulates that any city must be accessible by rail from any
   other (not necessarily directly) at all times.  In how many diﬀerent
   ways can some of the lines be closed subject to this condition?
