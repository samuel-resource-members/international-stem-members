```text
3. A 2019 × 2019 square grid is made up of 20192 unit cells. Each cell is coloured either black
   or white. A colouring is called balanced if, within every square subgrid made up of k2 cells
   for 1 ≤k ≤2019, the number of black cells differs from the number of white cells by at
   most one. How many different balanced colourings are there?

  (Two colourings are different if there is at least one cell which is black in exactly one of
   them.)
```
