## Text (OCR)

```text
ABCD is a tetrahedron with DA = DB = DC = d and
AB = BC = CA=e. M and N are the midpoints of AB
and CD. A plane 7 passes through MN and cuts AD
and BC at P and Q_ respectively.
(i) Prove that AP/AD = BQ/BC (=t, say)
(ii) Determine with proof that value of t, expressed in
terms of d and e, which minimises the area of the
quadrilateral MQNP .
```
