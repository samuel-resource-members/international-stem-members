## Text (OCR)

```text
3. Let ABC be an acute-angled triangle with AB > AC and BAC =
   60o. Denote the circumcentre by O and the orthocentre by H and let
  OH meet AB at P and AC at Q. Prove that PO = HQ.

Note: The circumcentre of triangle ABC is the centre of the circle which
passes through the vertices A, B and C. The orthocentre is the point of
intersection of the perpendiculars from each vertex to the opposite side.
```
