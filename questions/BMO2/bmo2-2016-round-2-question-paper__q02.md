## Text (OCR)

```text
2. Alison has compiled a list of 20 hockey teams, ordered by how good
   she thinks they are, but refuses to share it. Benjamin may mention
   three teams to her, and she will then choose either to tell him which
   she thinks is the weakest team of the three, or which she thinks is the
   strongest team of the three. Benjamin may do this as many times as
   he likes. Determine the largest N such that Benjamin can guarantee
   to be able to ﬁnd a sequence T1, T2, . . . , TN of teams with the property
   that he knows that Alison thinks that Ti is better than Ti+1 for each
   1 ≤i < N.
```
