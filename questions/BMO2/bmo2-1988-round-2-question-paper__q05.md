## Text (OCR)

```text
L and M are two skew lines in space, ie they neither meet nor are
parallel. A, B are the points on L, M respectively such that AB is
perpendicular to both L and M. Points P on L, Q on M vary so
that
P#A, Q#B, FQ is of constant length.
Show that the centre of the sphere through A, B, P, Q lies on a fixed
circle with centre the midpoint of AB.
```
