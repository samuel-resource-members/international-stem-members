```text
3. A block of mass m slides on a circular track of radius r whose wall and ﬂoor both have
   coeﬃcient of kinetic friction µ with the block. The size of the block is small compared to the
   radius of the track. The ﬂoor lies in a horizontal plane and the wall is vertical. The block is
   in constant contact with both the wall and the ﬂoor. The block has initial speed v0.





    (a) Let the block have kinetic energy E after traveling through an angle θ.  Derive an
                     dE
        expression for dθ in terms of g, r, µ, m and E.

    (b) Suppose the block circles the track exactly once before coming to a halt. Determine v0
        in terms of g, r, and µ.
```
