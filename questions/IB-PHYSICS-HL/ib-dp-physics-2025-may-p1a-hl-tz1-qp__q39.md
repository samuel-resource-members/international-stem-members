```text
39.   In a simple model of a nuclear reactor, four neutrons are emitted per fission on average.

    The average number of neutrons absorbed by the control rods is Nc per fission.

    The average number of neutrons that are lost through the walls of the reactor is N1 per fission.

    Any remaining neutrons induce further fissions.

    What are possible values for Nc and N1 for the reactor to maintain a steady reaction?

               Nc          N1

     A.        1           1

     B.        1           3

     C.        2           1

     D.        2           2
```
