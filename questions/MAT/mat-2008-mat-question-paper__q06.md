(i) A, B and C are three people. One of them is a liar who always tells lies, another is
a saint who always tells the truth, and the third is a switcher who sometimes tells the
truth and sometimes lies. They make the following statements:

A: I am the liar.
B: A is the liar.
C: I am not the liar.

Who is the liar among A, B and C? Brie y explain your answer.

(ii) P, Q and R are three more people, one a liar, one a saint, and the third a contrarian
who tells a lie if he is the  rst to speak or if the preceding speaker told the truth, but
otherwise tells the truth. They make the following statements:

P: Q is the liar.
Q: R is the liar.
R: P is the liar.

Who is the liar among P, Q and R? Brie y explain your answer.

(iii) X, Y and Z are three more people, one a liar, one a switcher and one a contrarian.
They make the following statements:

X: Y is the liar.
Y: Z is the liar.
Z: X is the liar.
X: Y is the liar.
Y: X is the liar.

Who is the liar among X, Y and Z? Brie y explain your answer.
