## Text (OCR)

```text
A. Whenever I toss a particular coin, it lands on heads with probability cos2 α for some
fixed real number α (and the outcome is independent of other tosses).  I toss the coin
three times. The probability that the coin lands on heads two or more times is equal to

  (a) 1 + 3 sin4 α −2 sin6 α,

 (b) 1 −3 sin4 α −2 sin6 α,

  (c) 1 + 3 sin4 α + 2 sin6 α,

 (d) 1 −3 sin4 α + 2 sin6 α,

  (e) 1 + 8 sin6 α.
```
