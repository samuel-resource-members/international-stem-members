7. For APPLICANTS IN COMPUTER SCIENCE ONLY.

Suppose we have a collection of tiles, each containing two strings of letters, one above the
other. A match is a list of tiles from the given collection (tiles may be used repeatedly)
such that the string of letters along the top is the same as the string of letters along the
bottom. For example, given the collection

                     A     ABA     CA

                      A    ABA    A

                                        ,              ,           ,             ,           ,               .
