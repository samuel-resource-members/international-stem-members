## Text (OCR)

```text
I. Given that there are positive real numbers a, b, c that satisfy

        Z b                           Z b
               logc  sin4 x tan2 x dx = 1  and       logc  sin2 x cos2 x dx = 3,
             a                                          a

it follows that the value of
                        Z b
                                      logc  sin4 x cos2 x dx
                                     a
must be equal to

  (a) 4,

 (b) 5,

  (c) 6,

 (d) 7,

  (e) 8.

[Note that sin4 x means (sin x)4.]
```
