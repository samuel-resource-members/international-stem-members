x-axis at the origin and at (a, 0) and at (b, 0) for some real numbers a < 0 and b > 0.
The curve only exists for α ⩽x ⩽β and for x ⩾γ. The three points with coordinates
(α, δ), (β, δ), and (γ, δ) are all on the curve.

   (i) What are the values of a and b?

  (ii) By completing the square, or otherwise, ﬁnd the value of δ.

 (iv) Find a cubic equation in x which has roots α, β, γ. (Your expression for the cubic
     should not involve α, β, or γ). Justify your answer.

  (v) By considering the factorization of this cubic, ﬁnd the value of α + β + γ.
