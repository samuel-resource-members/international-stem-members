## Text (OCR)

```text
H. Suppose that a function f(n) on the positive integers is defined such that f(1) = 1
and then for n ≥1

                f(2n) = f(n)  and  f(2n + 1) = f(n) + f(n + 1).

How many values of n are there such that f(n) = 3 and also n is a multiple of 35?

  (a) 0,

 (b) 1,

  (c) 2,

 (d) 3,

  (e) Infinitely many.
```
