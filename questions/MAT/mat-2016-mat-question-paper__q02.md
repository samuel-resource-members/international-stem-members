2. For ALL APPLICANTS.

   Let
                      A(x) = 2x + 1,    B(x) = 3x + 2.

(i) Show that A(B(x)) = B(A(x)).

(ii) Let n be a positive integer. Determine An(x) where

                         An(x) = A(A(A · · · A(x) · · · ).

                                         n times

Put your answer in the simplest form possible.

  A function F(x) = 108x + c (where c is a positive integer) is produced by repeatedly
applying the functions A(x) and B(x) in some order.

(iii) In how many diﬀerent orders can A(x) and B(x) be applied to produce F(x)?
Justify your answer.

(iv) What are the possible values of c? Justify your answer.

       Am1Bn1(x) + Am2Bn2(x) + · · · + AmkBnk(x) = 214x + 92     for all x?
