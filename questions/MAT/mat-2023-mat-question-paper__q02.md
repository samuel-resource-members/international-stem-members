2. For ALL APPLICANTS.

For n a positive whole number, and for x ̸= 0, let pn(x) = xn + x−n.

  (i) [3 marks] Sketch the graph of y = p1(x). Label any turning points on your sketch.

  (ii) [1 mark] Show that p2(x) = p1(x)2 −2.

 (iii) [1 mark] Find an expression for p3(x) in terms of p1(x).

 (iv) [5 marks] Find all real solutions x to the equation
