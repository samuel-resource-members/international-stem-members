H. Let p(x) = 2x4 −3x3 −5x2 +2x+2. Given that the y = mx, with m a real number,
crosses the curve y = p(x) at four distinct points, let the x-coordinates of those points
be x1, x2, x3, and x4. The product x1x2x3x4 is equal to

 (b) 1
