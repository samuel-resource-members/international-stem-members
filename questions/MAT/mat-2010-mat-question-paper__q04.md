The three corners of a triangle T are (0, 0), (3, 0), (1, 2h) where h > 0. The circle C has
equation x2 + y2 = 4. The angle of the triangle at the origin is denoted as θ. The circle
and triangle are drawn in the diagrams above for diﬀerent values of h.

(i) Express tan θ in terms of h.
(ii) Show that the point (1, 2h) lies inside C when h < √3/2.

(iii) Find the equation of the line connecting (3, 0) and (1, 2h) . Show that this line is
tangential to the circle C when h = 2/√5.
(iv) Suppose now that h > 2/√5. Find the area of the region inside both C and T in
terms of θ.

(v) Now let h = 6/7. Show that the point (8/5, 6/5) lies on both the line (from part
(iii)) and the circle C.
