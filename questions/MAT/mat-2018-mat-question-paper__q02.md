2. For ALL APPLICANTS.
Let S and T denote transformations of the ay-plane
S(z,y)=(e@+1,y),  T(a,y) = (-y, 2).
We will write, for example, 7S to denote the composition of applying S then T, that is
TS(a,y) = T(S(x,y)),
and write T” to denote n applications of T where n is a positive integer.
(i) Show that TS(x,y) # ST (x,y).
(ii) For what values of n is it the case that T(x, y) = (a, y) for all x,y?
(iii) Show that applications of S and T in some order can produce the transformation
U(a,y) = (w—1,y).
What is the least number of applications (of S and T in total) that can produce U?
Justify your answer.
(iv) Show that for any integers a and b there is some sequence of applications of S and
T that maps (0,0) to (a, 6).
(v) The parabola C has equation y = x? + 2x7 + 2.
What is the equation of the curve obtained by applying S to C?
What is the equation of the curve obtained by applying T to C?
