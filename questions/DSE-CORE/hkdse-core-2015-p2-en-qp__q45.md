## Text (OCR)

```text
Let $x_1,y_1$ and $z_1$ be the mean, the median and the variance of a group of numbers $\{a_1,a_2,a_3,\ldots,a_{50}\}$ respectively while $x_2,y_2$ and $z_2$ be the mean, the median and the variance of the group of numbers $\{a_1,a_2,a_3,\ldots,a_{49}\}$ respectively. If $x_1=a_{50}$, which of the following must be true?

I. $x_1=x_2$
II. $y_1\ge y_2$
III. $z_1\le z_2$

A. I and II only
B. I and III only
C. II and III only
D. I, II and III
```
