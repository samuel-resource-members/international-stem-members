## Text (OCR)

```text
Consider the following integers:

$2,2,3,3,3,3,3,5,5,6,8,8,9,10,m$.

Let $p,q$ and $r$ be the mean, the median and the mode of the above integers respectively. If $3\le m\le5$, which of the following must be true?

I. $p>q$
II. $p>r$
III. $q>r$

A. I only
B. II only
C. I and III only
D. II and III only
```
