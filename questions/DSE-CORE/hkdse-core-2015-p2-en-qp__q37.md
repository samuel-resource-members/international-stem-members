```text
Let $x_n$ be the $n$th term of a geometric sequence. If $x_6=216$ and $x_8=96$, which of the following must be true?

I. $x_3=729$
II. $\dfrac{x_5}{x_7}>1$
III. $x_2+x_4+x_6+\cdots+x_{2n}<2015$

A. I only
B. II only
C. I and III only
D. II and III only
```
