10. The coordinates of the points A and B are (5,7) and (13,1) respectively. Let P be a moving
point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that P is equidistant from A and B. Denote the locus
of Pby I.
(a) Find the equation of I. (2 marks)
(b) I intersects the x-axis and the y-axis at H and K respectively. Denote the origin by O.
Let C be the circle which passes through O, H and K. Someone claims that the
circumference of C exceeds 30. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. (3 marks)
