## Text (OCR)

```text
16 The reaction between aqueous iron(III) ions, Fe3+, and aqueous thiocyanate ions, SCN–, is
     reversible.

   The equation for the reaction is shown.

                              Fe3+(aq) + SCN–(aq)     FeSCN2+(aq)

                              yellow     colourless         red

    At equilibrium, the concentrations of Fe3+ ions, SCN– ions, and FeSCN2+ ions are identical, and
    the mixture appears orange.

   When a few drops of aqueous thiocyanate ions are added to the mixture, a new equilibrium
    forms.

   How does the colour of the mixture change?

   A    It becomes colourless.

   B    It becomes a lighter orange.

   C    It becomes more yellow.

   D    It becomes more red.
```
