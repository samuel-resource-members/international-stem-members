3. In Experiment 1, a student is given a resistor of unknown resistance and an air-filled parallel-
  plate capacitor of unknown capacitance. The student is asked to predict the expected time
  constant x of a circuit if these two circuit elements were connected in series with a battery.
  The student has access to a battery of known emf, a switch, an ammeter, a ruler, and wires, as
  shown in Figure 1. The plates of the capacitor are square, and the separation between the plates
   is small compared to the dimensions of the plates. The capacitor is initially uncharged. Assume
  that the dielectric constant of air is 1.

                                       Figure 1





  A. Describe a procedure for collecting data that would allow the student to determine the
     expected time constant x. In your description, include the measurements to be made.
     Include any steps necessary to reduce experimental uncertainty.
  B. Describe how the collected data could be analyzed to determine x. Include references to
     appropriate equations and to relationships between measured and known quantities.

C. In Experiment 2, the student is asked to determine the capacitance C of a new parallel-
   plate capacitor. For each trial, the absolute value TV of the potential difference across the
   capacitor is varied and the charge q stored on one plate of the fully charged capacitor is
  measured. Table 1 contains the data collected.

                                       Table 1
